## What it asks for, one line each

1. Shorter animations everywhere.
2. Every 3D model (Selena, her hand, Silverbolt, the Obsidia doll, the gramophone, the record crate) rendered in an anime style like Tetra and the drone: flat cel shading, a clean ink outline, saturated colour, no realistic PBR look.
3. The Obsidia doll is too big: make her smaller.
4. Silverbolt's normal head must sit in a straight line with its body, not bent down like a seahorse.
5. Watch every animation and fix what is ugly. Check everything and fix everything.
6. Add many live, dynamic backgrounds (the kind of shader backgrounds on reactbits.dev/c/backgrounds: aurora, beams, particles, silk, waves and so on), for example behind the Selena 3D section, and in many other places.
7. The animations must be trigger based, not scroll based: when the visitor reaches a scroll threshold the animation plays by itself, the scroll is locked until it finishes, and it must behave well scrolling down and scrolling up.
8. Check the UX of everything and make it good.
