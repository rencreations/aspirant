## What it asks for, by part

**Loader (first visit).** The drone smaller and in the centre of the screen, the "Touch anywhere to continue" text right under it. Fewer comic effects: only the necessary ones, none cut off by the screen edge.

**Intro and hero.** The drones set everything up in 2 seconds at most. The logo is stiff while carried and stands perfectly still once set (no wave at rest). The morph into the bar keeps its current wave. The splash cards show up fast, expanding outward from behind the logo (not out of a ring). Many more different pictures, like the splash art of `/collection/arts`, far fewer duplicates. The idle float of the cards and the scroll animation after it stay exactly as they are.

**The wall (aspirants).** More rows, smaller pictures, endless and repeating like premotio.com, lots of pictures, filling the whole left side including the top. A sharp frame edge: no fade to transparent. The words: a smaller Mobile Legends logo, the second line indented so the block reads left and right, a smaller TheMLabel logo with space either side, the whole block moved up a little to balance the drone at the bottom.

**Snap scrolling for the whole page.** Every section snaps: if the visitor stops mid scroll while an animation has not finished, the page finishes by itself, to the previous (or current) section or to the next. Sections with text and information need a scroll threshold: the visitor has to scroll more before the page moves on.

**The drone.** Remove all the dialogue boxes (speech bubbles) of the normal 3D drone.

**Rows (the section after the wall).** The rows keep scrolling by themselves, endlessly. Scroll input makes them go faster and they keep reacting as they do now. The words close to the right. At a threshold the transition fires: a video arrives in its own space between the pictures (never in front of them) and turns into the next section.

**Strip (the dark section).** A custom GLSL background in the spirit of psychoactive.co.nz, interactive with the cursor and in the site's design system, used until the "Official animations for The Aspirants" screen or the one after. The video in better quality; when it grows it must not be cropped: use the video's real aspect ratio. Titles and big words not italic. The CTA points to /collection/video/films. The main video is the whole film, streamed when previewed (not loaded whole at once), no lag, with slow connection and stall handling.
