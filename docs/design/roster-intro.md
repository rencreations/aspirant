# The roster intro and the redesigned roster page

The owner's brief for `/character` (30 Oct 2026): when a visitor opens the page, play a transition that is **exactly like** the reference recording (copied into the repository as [`roster-reference/transitions.mov`](roster-reference/transitions.mov), originally `C:\Users\muham\Videos\transitions.mov`, 9.5 s, 1920x1080, 60 fps, a screen recording of the game's collection page) or better, made in the browser with no video of the transition itself; and redesign the roster page to look like the end of that recording (frame outlines, shapes, mecha details, the red slash between Obsidia and Selena, the red Obsidia side, and the logo block with the text under it, which is ugly and badly placed now).

This file is the shared spec for the three pieces of work. Read it whole, then [HANDOFF.md](../HANDOFF.md) and the rules file at the repository root.

## The reference, second by second

The recording and contact sheets are in [`roster-reference/`](roster-reference/README.md). Frames: `ffmpeg -ss <t> -i docs/design/roster-reference/transitions.mov -frames:v 1 f.png`. `sheet0..3.jpg` and `dsheet0..3.jpg` are contact sheets of the first 4.7 s, `full_5.3.jpg`, `full_7.0.jpg` and `full_9.3.jpg` are full-size frames of the finished page, `welcomes.jpg` shows each character's welcome animation. The game's own top bar (back arrow, "The Aspirants", share, sound, full screen, "Brand Story", the two round icons) and the yellow "Full Collection Benefits" bar are the game's interface and are **not** copied: the site keeps its own bar (the sticky nav) and has no benefits bar. Everything else is copied or improved.

The recording is not smooth on purpose: the animation is cut on twos and threes (many consecutive frames are identical, then a jump), with hard cuts. Keep that stepped feel in the beam and the impact frames; keep the panel movement smooth.

## State at the end of the first working session (30 Oct 2026)

- **Built and pushed:** the redesigned page, the ten welcome clips, the engine, the shatter and impact ending (instead of the laser), the "only from another page" rule, the black first paint, and a minimal background (see below). Commits `8c67ad0` to `0285ef5` on `main`.
- **The owner's later decisions (they override the table):**
  1. The opening up to 2.8 s was approved ("great"). The beam ending was judged weird and replaced with a hit: charge, five impact frames of Obsidia, a boom with shattering glass, then her reveal. It does not have to follow the recording.
  2. It plays only when the visitor arrives from another page (for example `/` to `/character`) and on a fresh load or refresh. It does not play when coming from `/character/*` (`lib/route-history.ts`).
  3. The page must be black from the first paint, with no flash of the roster before the black.
  4. The background decoration (rulers, labels, bars, hazard stripes, data columns) was first packed in, then the owner said it was **too much**. It is now minimal: a thin ruler above and below, one line of type per pair, and a calm red side. Do not add more.
  5. Obsidia and Selena are shaded when not hovered, like the other eight, and clear on hover.
  6. The red slash between Obsidia and Selena has its widest part in the middle of the seam, not at the bottom.
  7. The pink line with a square at the top beside Obsidia's frame was removed.
- **Known gaps:** clip stepping and some panel beats before 2.8 s are estimates (fixed 20 or 30 steps per second per beat), the prism flare is procedural, the black swirl behind the beam at 3.3 to 3.5 s is not reproduced, Selena's panel is wider than in the reference, the decor text at 2560 and on a phone may overlap the nav logo and the "04 CHARACTERS" label (it was faint, and the decor was cut down afterwards, so look again), performance on a weak machine was not measured (the dev build held 60 fps on an RTX 4060 laptop with occasional 33 to 83 ms frames between 2 and 3 s while several clips decode), and the intro was compared against the recording at 1920x1080 only.

## Pieces of work

### 1. Assets (`scripts/roster-intro/`, `apps/web/public/video/roster-intro/`, `apps/web/public/img/roster-intro/`)

- One short clip per character from the game's welcome recording, `D:\ren\aspirant\media\game\animation\<slug>\welcome.mkv` (3840x1728, 60 fps, 16 to 25 s; slugs `angela change fanny guinevere layla lesley obsidia ruby selena vexana`). Choose the stretch each panel shows in the reference where it can be recognised (Fanny: the rooftop dive and the eye close-up; for the others pick the most dynamic 1 to 2 seconds), cut it, scale to 1600x720, 60 fps, H.264 (`-crf 28`, `+faststart`, no audio, keyframe every 12 frames so it seeks well), and put it in `public/video/roster-intro/<slug>.mp4`. Each clip should be well under 1.5 MB. Write `scripts/roster-intro/build.py` (run with `python -I` of the anaconda install) that cuts them from a table of start times so they can be recut, and the generated manifest `apps/web/src/features/character/roster/intro/clips.generated.ts`:

  ```ts
  export interface IntroClip {
    slug: Slug;
    src: string;
    from: number;
    duration: number;
    width: 1600;
    height: 720;
    focus: { x: number; y: number };
  }
  export const INTRO_CLIPS: Record<Slug, IntroClip>; // src is "/video/roster-intro/<slug>.mp4"
  ```

  `from` is the time in the _source_ the clip starts at (for the record), `focus` is where the interesting part of the frame is (fractions), used to crop it into a slanted panel.

- Stills taken from the reference and cleaned (the game's top bar removed, background keyed to transparent where the shape stands alone), as WebP with alpha under `public/img/roster-intro/`: the held beam (3.0 s), the pink beam (2.7 s), the blue beam (3.4 s), the thin beam (3.6 s), the red speed-line burst (2.5 s), the red energy slash (from `full_5.3.jpg`, along the diagonal), the black ring arcs (3.7 s), the fire and embers (3.6 s). Where something stands in front of the part that is needed (the game's bar, a panel), rebuild that part: first try to clone or inpaint it with code; use an image generator only when that is not possible (ask for a transparent background and check the alpha before using it). Document every file and where it came from in `scripts/roster-intro/README.md`.
- Everything the intro needs on the first screen goes into the loading list for `/character` (see the rules file: `lib/route-preloads.ts`). The clips are first paint (the intro starts at once), 10 clips of about 1 MB.

### 2. The engine (`apps/web/src/features/character/roster/intro/`)

A component `RosterIntro` mounted by the `/character` page. It plays on every arrival at `/character` (not on a scroll back inside the page), over the page, **below the site bar** (`--z-nav`), and at its end it is removed. It is made of:

- A **timeline** (`timeline.ts`): a pure function of time `t` (seconds, 0 to about 4.8) that returns what every layer is doing (panel states, beam state, flash level, and so on), so it can be tested and so a frame can be shown at any time. `window.__rosterIntro = { seek(t), play(), duration }` exists for the checks below, and `?introT=3.2` in the address freezes the intro at that time (and `?intro=off` skips it).
- A **renderer**: one WebGL canvas (three.js is already a dependency; a small hand-written renderer with GLSL is also fine) for the panels (video and splash textures, the slanted clip, the impact frame as a luminance threshold of the picture with an edge glow, the rainbow refraction fringe, the white glow outline, the flash, a chromatic split), plus layers for the beam (the stills of the assets above, moved and scaled in steps), the red wedge, the speed lines and the fire. Anything that can be done more cheaply with a DOM element and `transform` / `opacity` should be (the rules in the rules file: transform and opacity only in a DOM animation; no `filter` or blend over live video). The welcome clips are `<video>` elements, muted, `playsInline`, kept as textures, and seeked to the frame the timeline asks for (or played, whichever stays in time).
- **Geometry from the roster**: the panels of the intro are the roster's own slots (`SLOTS`, `BOARD_H` in `../layout.ts`) drawn where they would be if the board were scrolled to its far right and then panned back to the start, so the intro shows the real panels arriving in reverse order and its end lines up with the page underneath. When the roster's geometry changes, the intro follows because it reads `SLOTS`.
- **Hand-over**: the page below is the finished roster (piece 3). The root of the roster carries `data-intro="off|play|done"` (off when there is no intro). The engine sets it to `play` at the start (the roster hides its logo block and its idle effects) and to `done` at 4.2 s (they fade in by CSS transitions that the roster owns). The flash at 3.17 s covers the switch from the intro scene to the page; the later layers (the thin beam, the fire in Obsidia's panel, the black arcs, the red slash flash) are drawn by the engine over the real page until 4.2 s, in the screen position of Obsidia's panel (read from the DOM: the element with `data-roster-panel="obsidia"`).
- **Fallbacks**: reduced motion (the system setting or the site toggle) skips it and shows the finished page. `lib/perf.ts` level `lite` plays a short version (no video textures: splash art only, the same cuts and impact frames), `balanced` plays it without the rainbow and the fire. No WebGL: skip. Any click, tap or Esc skips to the finished page (a calm 200 ms fade). The visitor never waits for it: the page below is usable the moment the intro ends or is skipped.
- Tests: unit tests for the timeline (state at the key times of the table above), an e2e spec that opens `/character/?introT=<t>` for a few `t` and checks the canvas is there and `?intro=off` shows the page, and a check that the intro does not leave a running loop after it ends.
- A **frame comparison tool** (`scripts/roster-intro/compare.mjs`): opens the site at `/character/?introT=<t>` for a list of times, saves screenshots at 1920x1080, extracts the reference frame for the same `t`, and writes side-by-side images so the match can be judged by eye; use it all along. Keep going until each frame of the table is a close match, or better.
- Sound: the reference's audio track is silent; the intro has no sound.

### 3. The roster page (`apps/web/src/features/character/roster/`)

Redesign the page to look like the end of the reference (`roster-reference/full_5.3.jpg`, `full_7.0.jpg`, `full_9.3.jpg`), keeping everything it does now (sideways scroll and drag, the keys, the hover effects, links, the pair tags, the next-page controls, accessibility, the loading list, the tests; change a test only when the page it checks has really changed):

- The frame outlines and shapes: slanted panels with a thin outer pink or silver bevel, an inner dark line, short bright bars and small notches at the corners, a **more mecha** look (cut corners, panel seams, small tick marks and screws, thin double lines), the same lean on every shape.
- The red side: a deep red field with a dot grid on the left, the big Obsidia panel with a thin pink outline and a short bright bar on its top left edge, and the **red energy slash** on the diagonal between Obsidia and Selena (jagged, white-hot at the core, dark red cracks, with a slow pulse; build it with SVG or CSS and `transform` / `opacity`, or a small canvas), and the same kind of separator, in the colour of the pair, between the other panels where it fits.
- The logo block: the logo large at the top left, the line under it, the faint stat ticks and the emblem, **placed properly** (aligned to the panel edge, nothing overlapping the Obsidia panel, nothing cut by the screen on 1366x768, 1440x900, 1920x1080, 2560x1440 and on a phone at 390x844). Remove the old "Meet the ten" block, or fold its words (ten Aspirants, five pairs, 380 voices) into the small tech lines under the logo.
- Idle life: the pulse of red light on the Obsidia side and the slash (about 1.2 s period), a slow dim and return of the panels at the right, drifting dots. All with transform and opacity, and gated on `data-perf`.
- The entrance hooks for the intro: `data-roster-panel="<slug>"` on every panel, `data-intro` on the root as above (`[data-intro="play"] [data-intro-item]` is hidden, and `[data-intro="done"]` transitions the logo block in over 0.5 s, the stats 0.2 s later, and starts the idle effects), and the finished state must look right when `data-intro` is `off` or absent.
- Every change is checked by screenshots at the sizes above, read by eye.

## Rules for everyone

- Work only in your own folders; a shared file (a router, `lib/route-preloads.ts`, `nav`) is changed by the engine's author only, with a small edit.
- Do **not** commit, push or build `out/` (the integrator does). Run `npx tsc --noEmit -p apps/web`, `npx eslint <your folders>` and the tests of your folders. A dev server for looking is running on `http://localhost:3100` (never start another one, never kill node processes).
- Writing and code rules: the rules file and HANDOFF.md. No em or en dashes in any text, no mention of any assistant or tool in code, comments, docs or file names.
- Performance: judge on a production build only for speed; in dev just look. Keep the intro's code in a lazy chunk (a `chunkItem` in the loading list) so it does not weigh on the other pages.
