## 2. The main drone ("the normal drone")

### 2.1 How it is mounted

- **Raw three, not r3f.** One `WebGLRenderer({canvas, antialias:true, alpha:true, powerPreference:"high-performance"})`, `outputColorSpace = SRGBColorSpace`, `autoClear=false`, clear colour alpha 0. **No lights, no tone mapping**: every material is `MeshBasicMaterial` (loaded from `KHR_materials_unlit`) with `toneMapped=false`; the paint carries the shading.
- **One fixed full-viewport canvas**, made in code by `DroneLayer` (`drone-layer.tsx:122`), prepended into `.layer` (`drone-layer.module.css`: `position:fixed; inset 0; height:100lvh; z-index:var(--layer); overflow:hidden; pointer-events:none; contain:layout style`). The canvas takes no pointer. One round `<button>` (100px, scaled by transform to 0.84 of the drone's size) follows the drone for hover, press and keyboard; `data-cursor="HELLO"`.
- **Camera trick**: a 20 degree lens at distance 8 (`FOV=20, DISTANCE=8`, `VIEW_HEIGHT=2.821 m`). The drone never leaves the origin; its screen place is a **view offset** (`camera.setViewOffset(w,h, w/2 - x, h/2 - y, w, h)`), so it is never sheared at the edges. Size is `body.scale = (size/BODY_METRES) * VIEW_HEIGHT / height`. Attitude is `body.rotation.set(pitch, yaw, roll)` with order `YXZ`. So everything about the drone is **2D screen pixels plus three angles**: no depth, no real 3D relation to other actors.
- **Pixel ratio**: `min(devicePixelRatio, 2, sqrt(8e6 / (w*h)))` (`PIXEL_BUDGET = 8e6`). Same rule for everyone (one-version policy).
- **Draw order per frame**: `renderer.clear()`; flat trail scene (ortho, pixels, cyan `#23d0d8`, alpha 0.6 ribbon of up to 24 points, built per frame in a `DynamicDrawUsage` buffer) if `trail > 0.01`; then the drone. Before the first frame: `renderer.compile(scene,camera)`, `compile(flat)`, `initTexture` for each texture (no shader hitch on first flight).
- **Ink outline**: the skinned mesh again with `MeshBasicMaterial({color:#1f2735, side:BackSide})`, vertices pushed along `aSmooth` (position-averaged normals) by `uOutline = clamp(1.1, 2.4, size*0.0042) px / perMetre`. Keeps a constant pixel line at any size.
- **Lost context**: `webglcontextlost` is `preventDefault`ed, shell waits 1.5 s (`LOST_WAIT_MS`), rebuilds up to 3 times (`REBUILDS`), then `giveUp()` -> `engine = "none"` -> still picture `img/about/drone.webp` laid at the hero's place. No WebGL (`hasWebGL()` in `components/gl/use-gl-ready.ts`) -> same fallback.
- **Dispose**: `renderer.dispose(); renderer.forceContextLoss();` plus geometry, materials, textures.
- **Model load**: `loadMedia(DRONE_MODEL,{type:"model/gltf-binary"})` -> object URL -> `fetch` -> `GLTFLoader.parseAsync`. The preload list wires it: `chunkItem("about-drone","Waking the drone",()=>import("./drone/drone-scene"),3)` and `mediaItem(DRONE_MODEL,"Waking the drone",{type,bytes:250192,weight:1})`. The shell calls `expectReady("about-drone")` (`lib/page-ready.ts`) and marks it two animation frames after the first draw, so the curtain waits for a drawn drone.

### 2.2 How it reads scroll (director, `drone/director.ts`)

- Its **own `requestAnimationFrame` loop** (not the gsap ticker), for the whole life of the page, paused only when `document.hidden`. Scroll is `getLenis()?.scroll ?? window.scrollY` (`useLenis()` from `components/smooth-scroll.tsx`: Lenis on the gsap ticker, `autoRaf:false, lerp:0.12, touchMultiplier:1.2`).
- `dt = min(0.05, elapsed)` (0.1 during the opening). All springs are exact solutions, so they are frame-rate independent (a test checks 60 vs 144 Hz).
- Per frame: `target = path.at(scroll, stillFor > 0.45)` -> `layerStep` (which side of the film) -> `stepFollower` -> `stepTricks` -> `stepSpin` -> `floatOf` -> `stepRig` -> `stepFace` -> `scene.draw`. Then writes `stageRefs.drone = {x,y,size,presses}` and moves the button by `translate3d` + `scale`.
- Re-measure (`stale` flag) on `ResizeObserver` of the layer and the page root and on `document.fonts.ready`. Never per frame. `measure()` reads: layer client size, `probe.offsetHeight` (= `--nav-h`) and `probe.offsetWidth` (= `--gutter`), `measureAnchors(root)`.

### 2.3 Anchors (`drone/measure.ts`)

- Any empty `aria-hidden` element with `data-drone-anchor="<id>"`; its **centre** is the drone's centre and its **width** is the drone's width in px (`.anchor { aspect-ratio:1; pointer-events:none }` in `sections/kit.module.css:120`). Optional `data-drone-face="<one of 9 cells>"`, `data-drone-turn="<degrees>"` (yaw at rest).
- Returned as `AnchorBox {id, x, y (document coords), w, stick?:{from,to}, face?, turn?}`. Anchors inside a `position:sticky` box get `stick` (the scroll range over which the box holds), computed from the containing block (`heldRange`), so the path knows where the anchor is on screen for any scroll.
- Rest-yaw defaults: hero 48 deg (faces right and a bit to the visitor), perch -24, other anchors +-22 by side of screen.

### 2.4 Follower, rig, tricks, face (what the drone can do today)

- **Follower** (`choreo/follower.ts`): critically damped springs, omega 7.2 place, 6.4 size, 7.5 yaw, 8.5 pitch, 9.5 roll. Attitude comes from its own velocity: nose along the flight (yaw `clamp(vx*1.15, +-1.22)`, pitch `clamp(vy*0.8, +-0.72)`), bank `-vx*0.4 - ax*0.035` (+-0.66), blended by `fast = ramp(0.16, 0.85, speed in screens/s)`. A scroll up is a flight up nose first, not a rewind. `floatOf`: bob 0.026*size at 1.55 rad/s, sway 0.012*size, roll .035, pitch .03, scaled by `calm = 1 - fast`.
- **Rig** (`choreo/rig.ts`, 10 bones, posed by code in `drone-scene.ts:455`): `pose(part, [[axis, angle]...])` = rest quaternion times turns about the bone's own axes. Wings: spread (Z, mirrored sign), raise (X), twist (Y, mirrored sign); fins: stand (X), splay (Z mirrored); cyan strips (`antenna_*_02`, X). Wing beat phase rate 0.42 to 5.2 Hz by `drive`; depth 7 to 34 deg; flare (brake) opens wings wide; wings/fins trail the body on loose springs (`swingTo`) fed by acceleration. Fins perk up when `happy`.
- **Tricks** (`choreo/tricks.ts`): barrel .82 s, frontflip/backflip .86, spin .78, wiggle .72, loop 1.08 (travel-only, fixed order with .5 s air); answers to a press: hop .95, roll .9, nod .85, dizzy 1.6 (4 presses in 2.4 s). One-shots, always finish, refuse a second while one plays. `startTrick(tr, kind, dir)` is the hook for scripted moves. Each trick returns `{yaw,pitch,roll,dx,dy (in drone widths),effort,flare,face}`.
- **Face** (`choreo/face.ts` + shader in `drone-scene.ts:336`): the face is a 3x3 atlas (512 px square image, cell about 170 px). Cell offsets `CELL`: normal (0,0), angry (-1,0), squint (1,0) `> <`, happy (-1,-1) `^ ^`, dead (0,-1) `X X`, scan (1,-1), ecg (-1,-2), sleepy (0,-2), alert (1,-2) tall blades. The shader: `cell = floor(fuv*3 + (0,0.02))/3; local = (fuv-cell)*3 - uLook; local.y = 0.585 + (local.y-0.585)/max(1-uBlink,0.06); sample(cell + (uCell+local)/3)`. Cells cannot blend: a change is hidden behind a quick blink (`SWAP .15 s`). Own blinks every 1.5 + r*r*5 s, 24 percent double. Eye slide `EYE_REACH .085` cell, springs omega 16. `lookAt()`: body yaw `atan(dx)*.92` (+-0.72), pitch (-0.42..0.5), eyes +-1; gaze source is `stageRefs.gaze ?? mouse pointer` (touch ignored).
- **Press/hover** (`director.press/hover`): answers cycle hop, roll, nod; hover raises `glee` (fins up, happy face). The button stands down over any link/button of the page (`controlUnder`, `elementsFromPoint`, 4 times a second, only when still).

### 2.5 Path and stations (`choreo/path.ts`)

Stations in order: `hero`, `perch` (on the film window's top edge), `center` (statement), `dock`, then every other anchor. The **plain rule** (from the dock on): the drone stays with its anchor while it is in the band `BAND_IN .64 / BAND_OUT .36` of the screen, flies an arc (amplitude `min(.16*dist, .1*vw)`, side alternates, size +10 percent mid flight) to the next anchor (flight 0.12 to 0.5 screens of scroll), and when its anchor has left the screen it **draws back small (30 to 46 px) into the page margin on its own side ("the lot")**. A flight caught half way finishes to the nearer anchor after 0.45 s of rest (`settled`). Layering: `layerStep` flips the layer's `z-index` between `LAYER.drone=2` and `LAYER.film+1=4` only when the drone is clear of the film.

### 2.6 The opening (about only, `choreo/intro.ts`)

Pure function of time `introAt(geo, t)`: pass over the title 0 to 1.6 s (wavy Hermite line, barrel roll, trail), turn at the left edge 1.96 s, flip down, land 3.3 s with a real spring overshoot (`SETTLE 6.4`), done 3.95 s. Gated by `tryStart()`: `useSiteStore.entered && curtain.dataset.active !== "true" && !document.hidden && engine !== "pending"`. Skipped (straight to `done`) when `sessionStorage["aspirants:intro"] === "off"`, when `scrollY > 8`, or on any wheel/touch/key. `syncFollower()` lets the follower take over from a time-driven frame with no jump: **this is the pattern for any scripted sequence** (feed a pure `f(t)` into `syncFollower`, then hand back to `stepFollower`).

## 3. Guinevere's two drones (`A/companions/`)

### 3.1 Model (`public/models/about/companion.glb`, 241,420 B)

- **Not a skinned/rigged model.** 7 nodes, no skin, no animation: `companion` > `body`, `face`, `ear_L`, `ear_R`, `glove_L`, `glove_R`. Six rigid meshes, 2,246 vertices, 3,476 triangles, one 512x512 lossless WebP (the body paint), two materials `companion_body` and `companion_face`. Root extras: `radius`, `screen` (middle height and half width of the glass), `asModelled`.
- The "hands" are two **boxing gloves floating beside the ball** (orange-red, star on the back). There are **no arms and no bones**. The scene builds the model twice (two `Group`s sharing geometry and the picture).
- **The two drones differ only by resting face and size**: `bold` (slanted eyes, crooked dash) and `soft` (round eyes, cat mouth `w`), `SIZE [1, 0.9]`, different answer lists and RNG seeds. **No colour difference at all** (same texture, same gold/red/pink face constants). If home wants them told apart by colour, that is a new tint uniform.
- Face is not a picture: `companions/face.ts` draws it in the glass shader (SDF) from ~20 numbers (`FaceShape`: eye box w/h/roundness/gap/height, upper lid + slant + lower lid, arc/heart/spiral/chevron weights, mouth dash/cat/open/oh + width/tilt/height, blush, tone gold to red). Any face blends into any other; `faceDistance > 0.5` (different drawing) is changed behind a 0.2 s blink, otherwise blended over 0.16 s. `FACES`: bold, soft, bars, happy, glad, angry, wide (startled), dizzy (spirals), love (red hearts), squeeze (`> <` + open mouth, an effort or a laugh), flat, shy. I rendered all twelve on the live page: all read clearly at stage size.
- Pose frame (`pose.ts`): stage in CSS px, x right, y down, z toward the visitor (only decides who is in front, camera at z 4000). Glove position is in **radii of the ball** from the ball's middle (right, up, toward visitor), turn about its own axes, `size`. `ball.scale` = squash (volume kept), `lean` group = yaw/pitch/roll, `body.rotation.z = spin` rolls it like a wheel.

### 3.2 Performance vocabulary (`companions/performance.ts`)

- Structure: per actor a `Goal` (where each part wants to be) written every frame by `idle()` then overridden by a move or the story; `follow()` (line 1477) turns goals into poses with springs: ball omega `g.omega` (7 idle, 12 to 40 moves), yaw/pitch 13, roll `swingTo` 15/0.5, squash `swingTo` 26/0.45, ears `swingTo` 24/0.32 (+ drag from speed), gloves `springTo` or loose `swingTo` by `g.zeta` (<1 swings past), `free:true` gloves are in stage space and not turned with the ball, `g.jump` teleports.
- Helpers (all closures, not exported): `key(t, [[time,value,ease]...])`, `span`, `hump`, `EASE {io,out,in,back,lin}`, `setGlove`, `mixGlove`, `putAt(actor, glove, xPx, yPx, z, omega)` (**pins a glove to a stage pixel**), `near`, `facing`, `jab`, `guardUp`, `arc` (a thrown glove over a top point), `hit(t)` (fires a cue once when a clock crosses t).
- **Moves** (`SECONDS`): bold: combo 1.5, spin 1.4, flex 1.7, rocket 1.9 (one glove orbits the ball on a circle of radius 2.4 to 3.3 radii and is caught), fume 1.6; soft: hide 2.3, bounce 1.8, behind 3.1 (hides behind the other, which plays `guard` 3.1), hello 1.7, pout 1.6; both: dizzy 2.3, bump 2.5 (meet in the middle, fist bump), cheer 1.7, greet 2.2, jabs 1.1, flick .9. Press cycles each list; 4 presses in 2.4 s = dizzy then fume/pout; two pressed within 2.6 s = bump. Hover: bold squares up (`glad`), soft shrinks behind its gloves (`shy`).
- **Scripted story** (22.5 s clock, `ACT2 4.3`, `ACT3 16.2`): bold rolls in like a wheel behind a lead glove, soft peeks round the edge, they find the page's drone, fist bump with it, copy its roll, throw a glove over its head (`arc`, `thrown`), wave, zip off top right, come back. Cues drive the page drone via `env.askDrone("hop"|"nod"|"roll")` and `perf.eyes()` (where the drone should look).
- **Comic marks** (`companions/fx.ts`): `bang, ask, spark, heart, lines, drop (sweat), puff, swirl, ha, vein, star`; tones ink/pink/gold/cyan; DOM `<span>` with an inline SVG, played once by Web Animations (transform + opacity), removed on finish, max 18 live, 420 to 1400 ms. `createFx(host, className)`: the class must give `position:absolute; top:0; left:0; pointer-events:none; will-change:transform,opacity` (`companions-stage.module.css:120`). `hold()/advance()` freeze marks for probes. Colour tokens use `var(--text)`, `var(--accent)`, `var(--bg)` (puff fill).
- **What they can already do that the brief wants**: struggle (squeeze face, `offer()` tremble `sin(s*44)`, squash oscillation, `vein`/`drop` marks), pull (gloves pinned by `putAt` and moved apart), crank (a glove on a circle, as `rocket`), dance together (mirrored `spin`, `bounce`, `bump`, `cheer`), throw/catch (`arc`), watch/confusion (look target + `wide`, `ask` mark, `shy`), wave (`hello`, `greet`), exit (crouch + zip, `jump` teleports), idle bob/blink/look at pointer/other/page drone.

### 3.3 Stage shell (`companions-stage.tsx`)

Own canvas inside the stage box (not fixed), `NEAR 120%` asks for the script and model, `FAR 280%` **releases the scene and the WebGL context**, runs only while on screen and the tab is visible, scene starts at 60 percent visible (waits 1.6 s for the page drone), buttons (one `<button>` per drone, `data-cursor` SPAR / HELLO) follow each ball. Probe: `window.__aboutCompanions` {state, freeze, advance(s), replay, skip, press(who), hover(who,on), play(kind,who), face(who,name|null), pretendDrone(on)}.

## 4. Tetra (`A/tetra/`)

### 4.1 Model (`public/models/about/tetra.glb`, 573,716 B)

- Chibi doll, 0.649 m tall, feet on the ground, faces +Z. **Skinned, Mixamo-named, 37 bones** (cut from 96): `Hips`, `Spine/Spine1/Spine2`, `Neck`, `Head`, `LeftShoulder/Arm/ForeArm/Hand/HandIndex1/HandThumb1` (and Right), `LeftUpLeg/LeftLeg` (and Right), 15 spring bones (`hair_back_L_01/02`, `hair_side_*`, `hair_bang_C_01`, `ear_L`, `ear_R`, `skirt_*`). **No feet, no toes** (legs end in a point), **no skinned fingers** (hands are mittens), no IK or socket bones (the rig file's IK chains and `head_look_target` were dropped).

### 4.2 Scene (`tetra-scene.ts`)

- Per frame, in this exact order: every non-spring bone back to rest; clip sampled by hand (no mixer) with `weight` (slerp/lerp); the `Turn` channels laid on top (`TURNED` table at line 87: hips, spine=Spine1, head = Neck 35 percent + Head 65, armL/R, foreL/R, legL/R = UpLeg); a fixed `SQUARE` correction on the head; actor place `(x,y,z)` in metres from her mark and `(pitch,yaw,roll)` order YXZ; squash done in the vertex shader after skinning (`uSquash`, so bones and springs never see it); springs (hair, ears, skirt) last at fixed 60 Hz; morph weights; then **marks** projected back to canvas px: `head, crown, chest, ground, handR, handL, scale` (px per metre).
- **Never scale her model**: the springs run in world metres. On-screen size comes from camera distance via `StageView.tall` (px): `distance = height / (2*tan(FOV/2)*perMetre)`, FOV 20, 7 degrees look-down, `setViewOffset` slides the mark to `(markX, groundY)`. Same ink outline as the drone (`INK #2a3342`, 1.1 px).
- `Turn` is in radians about **her own axes**: x her left, y up, z forward. Arm z raises sideways (+ for her left, - for her right), arm x negative swings forward.

## 8. Gotchas (read before touching the engine)

1. **`completeLayout` discards home's own anchors.** It always injects `hero, perch, center, dock` from `windowBox`/the reel (about's film) and keeps a measured extra anchor **only if `a.y > dock.y`**, where the default dock is about `(TRACK_SCREENS + 1.2) * vh` down. Using `buildPath` on home would silently drop the first four screens of anchors. Home needs its own path; only the plain-anchor rule (`BAND_IN/OUT`, lot, `settled`, arc) carries over, extracted from `schedule`, `place`, `route` (the `i >= 3` branches).
2. **The about director waits for the gate** (`entered`, curtain not active). A loader drone cannot be it; it needs its own loop outside `#site-shell`.
3. The two Guinevere drones have **identical paint**; they differ only by face, size 0.9 and move lists.
4. **Tetra's context is never released**; companions' is (280 percent). The page ceiling is 3 live contexts.
5. **Never scale Tetra's model**; fixed 60 Hz spring steps; call `scene.settle()` after a pause or a jump of the pose. `Springs` must be constructed with the model unmoved in rest pose.
6. The drone's frame rate and fps numbers above are from `pnpm dev`, not authoritative.
7. Home wants many actors (drone, duo, Tetra, doll, Silverbolt, Selena, gramophone, hand): **one canvas and one context per actor will not fit** the three-context rule and fullscreen MSAA buffers are the dominant GPU cost. Way out: one shared renderer. Each scene's `assemble(renderer, model, canvas, hooks)` already takes the renderer; change it to take a renderer and a scene and not own the canvas or the context-loss listeners, keep one camera per scene, `autoClear=false`, draw order = z order (`clearDepth` between). Use two canvases (back and front) only if DOM must sit between layers.
8. Every scene builds shaders with `onBeforeCompile` and `customProgramCacheKey` ("companion-face", "companion-hull"): keep unique keys when cloning materials, or programs collide.
9. Face-name and mark-kind collisions across modules (see section 0).
10. Rules that bind any home work (the project rules file at the repo root): transform and opacity only around the canvas; no `filter`/`backdrop-filter`/`mix-blend-mode` over the live picture; no per-frame writes to `<html>`/`<body>` or SVG attributes; no scroll cue in a join; **one version only** (no lite scene, no device-gated effect; a no-WebGL fallback is a failure handled, not a version); anything needed on first paint goes in `lib/route-preloads.ts` as a `PreloadItem` (the drone model and its chunk are already one: `aboutItems()`), the rest `deferred`; no timers that fake progress.
11. Handedness: companions' `earL`/`gloveL` are on the **right of the screen** when the drone faces us; Tetra's `armL` is her left, also the right of the screen when she faces us. The drone's wing and fin Y/Z turns need opposite signs per side.
12. `createDroneScene` calls `loadMedia(DRONE_MODEL,{type})` without `expectedBytes`, the other two pass it; harmless but means a saved copy of a wrong size is not detected for the drone.
13. The home route is `app/page.tsx` today (imports `features/home/*`); it must be replaced wholesale.
