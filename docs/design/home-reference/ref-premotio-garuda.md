## 0. The ten facts to know before reading anything else

1. Both sites draw all their cards in ONE fixed full-screen WebGL canvas behind the DOM, driven by one scroll progress number. Cards are real 3D planes in a pixel-matched perspective camera.
2. Premotio = a recent three.js (WebGL2, reversed-depth path; revision not read), instanced bendable cards, 20 cards from 9 pictures, plus a full-screen post pass that makes the "chromatic aberration": a directional multi-tap smear with a red-to-blue weight ramp per tap. NOT an RGB-offset. Plus a light pointer "wake" (a 160px half-float velocity field) that drives the wave-like cursor distortion.
3. THE GATHER IS A SPIRAL IN THE RING'S OWN CYLINDRICAL FRAME (the motion the owner wants 1:1). The hero is a continuous emission of cards (confetti thrown out of a ring at the screen centre, tumbling in turbulent air); its clock slows to a stop in the first 4% of the story (inferred from `freeze: [0, 0.04]` and the `1 - smoothstep(freeze)` speed factor, not traced to the call site). Then each card, picked in order of its polar angle around the ring axis (the ring is tilted 22 deg toward the viewer), is interpolated in cylindrical coordinates (angle, radius, height) about that axis, NOT in x,y,z: its polar angle sweeps (quintic, matched to the ring's own angular pace and acceleration) to its slot angle while its radius shrinks from where it is to 0.75 R with a smoothest-step and its height falls to 0. A sweeping angle plus a shrinking radius is a visible spiral into the circle. Then the ring releases cards one at a time (largest angular gap first, in angular order) and each flies a Cartesian quintic Hermite to its wall slot. The "fly to the bottom" the owner sees is the cards converging toward the lower centre before the ring forms; the ring settles near screen centre. See 1.7 for the exact mapping.
4. Premotio scroll story length: 3 units where 1 unit = 1.4 x innerHeight (1260 px at 900). Hero 0-151 px frozen, gather 151-1285, ring hold to 1588, land on the wall 1588-3553, text reveal 3024-3667, wall pinned about 3780-5443.
5. Premotio wall: plane rotated twist 35 deg, tilt 53 deg, roll -6 deg, viewed through a telephoto (lens factor 2), alternate rows slide in opposite directions (240 px per row per unit of slide). Our owner wants the slide automatic (time based), not scroll based.
6. Garuda = three.js WebGPURenderer with TSL node materials (forced WebGL2 path is a URL flag). Cards are 4:5 (0.8) posters, 24x3 segment quads, GSAP timeline intro, Lenis lerp 0.085, ScrollTrigger pin with scrub. Depth of field is faked by cross-fading each card between its sharp texture and a pre-blurred "soft" texture (padded canvas blur), per card, by distance from the focus plane. Very cheap and very effective, and we should copy the idea.
7. Garuda hero layout is a hand-authored table (28 rows, 3 depth bands: near 2.45-2.7 fully blurred, mid 3.7-4.8 sharp, far 6.4-8.0 blurred) with fixed Euler tilts (x +-14, y +-30, z +-14 deg). Idle float is tiny (sin amplitudes 0.025 to 0.03 world units, 0.05 rad). Pointer parallax is depth-proportional.
8. Garuda "spiral" = a HELIX strip of posters around the central trophy, scroll slides cards along the helix (radius 1, pitch -0.22 per radian, 24 posters, card pitch 0.65 rad). Before that, the posters stand in a 36-slot ring around the trophy (the rolodex) then "uncoil" along a Catmull-Rom path into the helix. The intro (7 s) is two rows that bend into two concentric rings, spin, then fly out to the hero table.
9. Neither site has a per-card hover raycast in the hero. Hover is a force field (premotio) or camera/card parallax (garuda). Per-card hover/highlight in our hero is our own addition.
10. Our asset fit: `D:\ren\aspirant\media\_all\splash-art\cropped\` = 8 portrait 4:5 images (1152x1440; Vexana 973x1216), `normal\` = 10 wide 16:9 (2560x1440). 4:5 equals Garuda's card shape exactly. The owner's wall wants wide cards: morph the card aspect and crop between hero (4:5) and wall (16:9), see 3.4.

### 1.3 Scroll smoothing (important for feel)

Raw scroll goes through Lenis (lerp 0.1), then the scene progress passes through a critically damped spring (omega 18 rad/s) and the lag is soft-clamped: `lag = raw - shown; |lag| <= knee 0.08 U passes through, beyond that tanh-compressed to a maximum of 0.16 U`. So the scene never trails the real scroll by more than 0.16 U (200 px). Reproduce this as one function; it is what makes the flights feel weighty but never late.

### 1.4 Card mesh and shaders (implementable facts)

- One instanced quad, 28 x 28 segments. Per-instance attributes: position (3), quaternion (4), shape (w,h + picture crop x,y), paper (bend, axis, flutter, wave phase), lens, tube (ring params), way, look (corner, clip, haze, shows), drum, depth range, texture layer. Pictures live in one 2D array texture (all 9 same size). Every card is one instance in one draw call.
- Paper bend in the vertex shader: `across` = vertex coordinate along the bend axis; with bend b: `r = width / b; tilt = across / r; x = r sin(tilt); z = r (1 - cos(tilt))`. Flutter: a half sine wave along the longer side, `z += flutter * sin(length * PI / maxDim + phase)` ("it sways, never folds into an S"). Normal is recomputed from the same terms.
- Bend and flutter are driven by the card's velocity (spring-smoothed, `follows 0.08 s`): `bend = tanh(-vz / 2400) * 1.1`, `flutter = tanh(speed / 2400) * 0.05`, phase advances `6 + 12 * min(1, speed/2400)` rad/s. Both springs: critically damped-ish, `takes 10`, `lets 5`, `swing 4`.
- Fragment: rounded corner by a signed distance (corner radius as a share of width; wall corner 0.05), a hair of edge shade (`rgb *= 1 - 0.14 * (1 - smoothstep(0, 1.4, insidePx))`), a single directional light from the upper left front `normalize(-0.35, 0.5, 0.8)`, `dim = 1 - shade * (1 - smoothstep(-0.1, 0.8, dot(n, light)))`, edge alpha softened over one pixel (no MSAA), back face shows the same picture mirrored. Opaque cards write their own depth (card order = draw order, so overlapping cards never z-fight; `gl_FragDepth` is set from a per-card slot plus the within-card depth).
- Camera: `PerspectiveCamera` at (0,0,1000) looking at the origin with `fov = 2 atan(H/2/1000)` so world units equal CSS pixels at z = 0. near 10, far 40000. Constant `V = 1000`.
- Card size: poster height `325 * clamp(min(w,h)/900, 0.55, 1.15)` px at z = 0, times `near` in [0.7, 1.2] per card (random per card).

### 1.5 The hero flow and the pointer force (physics, simplified)

- Each card `n` has a rail heading `angle = n * 2.39996 (golden angle) + rand(+-0.1)`. It is born in the ring hole (`R = clamp(0.312 * min(w,h), 110, 350)` px, tube radius `a = clamp(0.11 * min(w,h), 39, 125)`), rounds the inner side of the ring tube (only visible once it passes the tube's inner side), then leaves tangentially toward the camera at 50 deg and flies on a precomputed flight (a 120-sample table of orientation, slip, press, rush from a 480-step integration of a paper-like body in an air field of 4 swirling plane-wave modes: flow 1000 px/s, gusts 0.35) until it exits the screen edge. Birth interval `= life_avg / count`, divided by 1.8 for the first 2.6 s of story time, and the simulation clock itself runs 6x fast at the start easing to 1x over 2.5 s of story time (`warm()` also pre-runs it), so the hero is already full at first paint.
- Pointer force on each card: radius `max(190, 0.42 * min(w,h))` px (378 at 900), falloff `w = (1 - (d/R)^2)^2`, push `= w * (dir * (1950 + 4.4 * min(speed, 2000)) + vel * 4.8 * min(speed,2000)/speed)`. Cards near the ring hole are less affected: `weight = 0.08 + 0.92 * smoothstep(0.35 o, 1.45 o, dist)` with `o = R + a`. Force is low-passed (`1 - exp(-dt * 8)`), integrated into a position offset by a damped spring (`omega = 2 pi / 1.15`, `zeta 0.56`). A second spring set tilts each card (x, y, z Euler offsets; `omega = 2 pi / 1.5`, `zeta 0.45`) from the offset velocity and the torque about the pointer: `turn.x = spring(S * fh(-(vy*0.003 + dy*0.0008)))` etc, soft-clamped by `0.6 * tanh(x/0.6)`. Fast flicks "throw" the card: `thrown` rises toward `smoothstep(0, 520, offsetSpeed)` and adds `+1.5 bend`, `+0.05 flutter` (the card curls like paper). The curl axis turns to the direction of travel. All fixed-step at 120 Hz sub-steps.
- Pointer velocity fed to the system is smoothed: `v += (raw - v) * 0.35` per frame.
- No raycast, no per-card hover state. (Their `hover` flag only enables a small pointer-follow tilt for the phone model further down.)

### 1.6 The post pass (this is the "chromatic aberration" and the cursor wave)

One full-screen quad reads the card render (RGBA, transparent where no card) and a small flow texture and writes the final frame over the paper colour `rgb(245,245,247)`.
Inputs: `sheets` (card render), `mask` (a coarse binary "is there any card here" map, 32 px cells, used to skip empty rays), `wake` (flow field, below), `ring = (R, a)`, `centre`, `appear`, `prism`, `edges`, `pixel` (DPR).
Per pixel (pseudo-code, my wording, formulas are theirs):

```
p        = uv * size
c        = p - centre ; dist = |c| ; dir = c / dist ; shorter = min(size.x, size.y)
// global lens: everything seen larger in the middle
pull     = 0.20 * appear * exp(-(dist/shorter)^2 / (2 * 0.42^2))
flow,curl= wake(uv).xy , .z ; speed = |flow|
seenAt   = c * (1 - pull) - flow * 0.011 * pixel          // pointer wake shifts the sample
d        = |seenAt|
// ring band: smear strongest just inside the ring tube
x        = (d - R) / a
near     = prism * exp(-((x + 1) / 0.6)^2)
// frame edge band
e        = abs(2*uv - 1) ; far = edges * smoothstep3((max(e.x,e.y) - 0.8) / 0.2)
reach    = a * near * (0.24 + 0.22) + shorter * far * (0.028 + 0.03)     // pixels
stretch  = dir * reach
if (speed > 1) { drawn = min(speed * 0.03, shorter/pixel * 0.035) * pixel ; stretch += (dot(way,dir)<0 ? -way : way) * drawn }   // wake streak along the flow
// spectral smear: N taps along the stretch centred on the pixel
N        = clamp(ceil(reach / (3*pixel)), 4, 16)
grain    = interleaved gradient noise(gl_FragCoord)         // per pixel start offset, so steps read as grain not bands
for i in 0..N-1: t = (i + grain)/N ; col = tap(seenAt + stretch*(t - 0.5)) ; k = spectrum(t)
                 sum += col.rgb * k ; cover += col.a * k ; w += k
out      = sum / w                                           // normalised: no brightness change
// spectrum(t): red at t=0, green at 0.5, blue at 1, mixed 85% with white:
//   c = clamp(abs(mod(t*4 + vec3(0,4,2), 6) - 3) - 1, 0, 1) ; k = mix(vec3(1), c, 0.85)
// "light in the ring hole falls on the sheets alone": cards come out of white, page stays page
light    = appear * exp(-(d / max(0.8*(R - a), 1))^2)
rgb      = paper * (1 - cover) + mix(out, cover, light)
// colour split at the pointer: where the flow rises or falls the 3 channels part
parted   = vec3(|wake(uv + flowDir*20px)|, speed, |wake(uv - flowDir*20px)|)
rgb     += 0.25 * exp(-|(uv*size - nose) / (0.08*shorter)|^2) * (parted - mean(parted)) / 1.0   // nose = pointer position in px
rgb     *= 1 + 0.035 * clamp(curl / 500, -1, 1)              // glint where the flow curls
rgb     += (noise - 0.5)/255                                 // dither
```

- When `reach < 0.75 px` there is no smear (single tap). When no card is within the stretch (mask test) the pixel returns paper. Smear longer than 4-8 px is computed in a half-resolution pass and blended in (an optimisation, keep it).
- `prism` (ring-band smear) is on in the hero and the gather: `prism = appear * (1 - 0.55 * g) * (1 - land)` with `g = smoothstep(p, 0.04, 0.14)` and `land = smoothstep(p, 0.42, 0.94)`, so cards near the ring band are smeared in the hero, the smear drops to 45% once the carousel is formed, and to 0 as the wall lands. `edges` = 1 in the hero, falling to 0.35 on the wall. `appear` ramps with `smoothstep3(O / 1.1)` over the first 1.1 s after load and is multiplied by `(1 - smoothstep(lens range 0..0.2))`.
- Wake field (the "wave following the cursor"): ping-pong half-float texture, size 160 px on the long side (aspect kept), one fragment pass per frame (`dt` capped at 0.1 s):

```
uv'   = uv - flow(uv) * dt / frameSize                       // semi-Lagrangian advection
c     = flow(uv')
gath  = ( E.x - 2c.x + W.x + 0.25*(NE.y - NW.y - SE.y + SW.y) ,
          N.y - 2c.y + S.y + 0.25*(NE.x - NW.x - SE.x + SW.x) )   // gradient of divergence: spreads and rolls up (this is the "wave")
c     = (c + 0.2 * gath) * exp(-dt / 0.35)                   // viscosity-free decay, 0.35 s
// stirring: a capsule from last pointer position to this one, gaussian across, radius = 0.05 * min(w,h) px
along = clamp(dot(p - from, stroke) / dot(stroke, stroke), 0, 1) ; d = |p - from - stroke*along|
c    += (force - c) * (1 - exp(-dt * 22)) * exp(-d^2 / radius^2)
out   = vec4(c, (E.y - W.y) - (N.x - S.x) /*curl*/, 1)
```

`force = pointerVelocity * min(1, 1700 / speed)` (px/s, y flipped). Pointer moves slower than 12 px/s do not stir. The field stays "live" 2.45 s (7 x 0.35) after the last stir, then the pass is skipped. Needs `EXT_color_buffer_float` or half float; if neither, the effect is off (their fallback; ours must not need one: half float render targets are universal on WebGL2).

### 1.7 Ring, schedule and wall (the scripted formation)

Carousel (ring): `inner 0.5, above 22 deg, nearest 2.6, wide 0.4, high 0.32`. `radius = min(0.4 * W, 0.32 * H, 1000/2.6 = 384.6)` (288 px at 1440x900). Slots sit on a circle of radius `0.75 * radius`, evenly spaced `2 pi / n` (n = number of cards, 20), faces outward, card width `0.5 * radius` (144), the ring's up axis is the Y axis rotated 22 deg about X. Ring angle as a function of p: ease-in ramp over the first 20% of the gather (`u = 0.2 * (0.34 - 0.04)`), then constant angular speed for a total of half a turn (`gathers 0.5`) across the gather; it keeps turning during the hold and while cards leave (leave order is by angle, spaced evenly in p over `0.34..0.94`, each in flight 8 slot-spacings long).
GATHER mapping (function `A` inside the scene timeline; this is what to implement). Work in the ring frame: axis `a` = Y rotated 22 deg about X, `front`, `side` = X. For a card with hero position `x`: `angle0 = atan2(dot(x, side), dot(x, front))`, `out0 = hypot(dot(x, front), dot(x, side))`, `up0 = dot(x, a)`. Its slot angle at time s is `slot(s) = 2 pi k / n + turn(s)` (the ring keeps turning). Pick time `u_i`, seat time `u_i + 0.13` (p units), latch window `l = max(latchFraction, 0.35)` of the flight, flight progress `o` in 0..1. `wrap = round((slot(u_i) - angle0) / 2 pi)` so the sweep takes the nearest way. During `o < l` (i = o / l): `theta = h + (slot(end) - h) * (10 i^3 - 15 i^4 + 6 i^5) + pace * (-4 i^3 + 7 i^4 - 3 i^5) + accel * (0.5 i^3 - i^4 + 0.5 i^5)` where `h = angle0 + 2 pi wrap`, `pace = turn'(end) * 0.13 * l`, `accel = turn''(end) * (0.13 l)^2` (a quintic Hermite in angle matching the ring's velocity and acceleration at latch); after `l` theta simply equals the slot angle at the current time (the card is riding the ring). Radius `r = lerp(out0, 0.75 R, smoothest(o ^ pw))` with `pw = log(latchFraction) / log(l)` when the card starts outside the clearance circle (so it clears its neighbours by the latch), else 1. Height `= lerp(up0, 0, smoothest(o))`. Position `= centre + r * (cos theta * front + sin theta * side) + height * a`. Add the live hero drift `(heroLive - heroAtPick) * (1 - smoothest(o))`. Orientation: slerp from the hero orientation to the slot orientation (faces outward from the axis, up = axis) by `smoothest(o / l)`, plus the ring's own spin; size, bend, flutter lerp with `smoothest(o)`. `smoothest(x) = x^4 (35 - 84x + 70x^2 - 20x^3)`. Leave order: sort cards by polar angle, rotate the list to start after the largest gap between neighbours, and assign increasing pick times in that order (matched to slots to keep each card's sweep short).
LAND (ring to wall), per card `t` = 0..1 over its flight: position = Cartesian quintic Hermite, in the wall plane's own frame (x, y in plane, z out of plane), from the ring slot (matching ring velocity and acceleration) to wall slot; z lies flat by `lies 0.55` of the way; a sway of 6 deg at `1.2` swings; drift 0.04; spin 0.25; orientation slerps ring-orientation to wall-orientation with `smootherstep(t / 0.55)`. Easing helper used everywhere: `smootherstep(x) = x^3 (6x^2 - 15x + 10)`, and `x^4 (35 - 84x + 70x^2 - 20x^3)` (smoothest step) for long ones.
Slot assignment: each card is assigned to the carousel slot (and each leaving card to a wall slot) that minimises angular / screen distance, avoiding neighbours showing the same picture; this avoids crossing paths. Do the same (Hungarian or greedy on projected distance, see Garuda 2.3 which uses Kuhn-Munkres).
Wall (`r_`): `twist 35 deg, tilt 53 deg, roll -6 deg, size 0.3, shape 0.7071 (w/h), gap 0.14, corner 0.05, slide 240`.

- `q = Rz(roll) * Rx(-tilt) * Rz(twist)` (first twist about Z, then tilt back about X, then roll).
- Wall frame (the left box): `size = 0.3 * sqrt(frameW * frameH)` is the card height (about 239 px for a 706x900 box), width = `0.7071 * size`; column pitch `= size * 0.7071 * 1.14`, row rise `= size * (1 + 0.7071 * 0.14)`; rows fill the projected frame, each row's length `= count * pitch`, rows alternate parity: even rows use the even pictures, odd rows the odd ones (no horizontal neighbours with the same picture). Each row wraps (modulo its length) so offset slides endlessly.
- Rows slide by `offset_r = way_r * 240 * slide`, `way = +1 / -1` by row parity (opposite directions). `slide` is scroll-driven in their site (a spring-smoothed function across the pinned 1.4 U). Ours: a constant speed in px/s (or per second), two directions alternating.
- The wall is seen through a telephoto: plane centre at `z = V - 2V`, i.e. 2000 px in front of a camera whose lens factor is 2; keep the perspective mild (equivalent FOV about 25 deg) but visible: the top row cards are slightly smaller than the bottom row.
- Wall clip: a rectangular scissor in screen space (`mask` 0.7-0.97) that closes the cards to the left box so they never run under the text; the wall frame is pixel-aligned with a DOM element (`data-story="wall"`), read each frame with `getBoundingClientRect` minus the canvas offset.

### 1.9 Implementation recipe for our site (premotio part)

Modules (all in `features/home/hero-field/` or similar, one WebGL canvas fixed behind the DOM; load three lazily, declare chunk + textures in `route-preloads.ts`, call `expectReady`):

### 2.5 Ring and helix maths (this is the "spiral around a central subject")

Ring (`rg = {n: 36, r0: 0.46, h: 0.8, y: -0.04, tilt: 0.24}`; phone `{30, 0.38, 0.8, -0.62, 0.3}`): slot k has angle `th = ringTurn + ringSpin + 2 pi k / n` where `ringSpin` integrates `ringVel + 0.06 * (1 - uncoil)` rad/s (idle turn of 3.4 deg/s), `ringVel` is the drag inertia (`*= exp(-1.6 dt)`). Radius `a = r0 + h * 0.8 / 2 = 0.78`. Position `(a sin th, 0, a cos th)` rotated by tilt (0.24 rad about X, bank about Z), `y += -0.04`. Card faces outward; the face is flipped when it would face away from the camera (`rflip`). Unused slots are filled with posters not adjacent to a duplicate. Card pose in gather: from hero pose to ring slot with `D = easeInOutCubic(clamp(gather * 1.3 - (k/n) * 0.3))` (so slots fill in order around the ring), position `lerp(c, ringPos, D)` then rotated about the world Y axis by `1.25 sin(pi D)` rad (a swirl on the way, the card arcs around the trophy instead of going straight), orientation slerp plus yaw unwrap.
Slot choice for gather: try both rotation directions and every offset 0..n-1 of the first card; pick the (offset, direction) that minimises the sum of screen-space distance between each hero card and its ring slot (brute force 2 x 36 x 24, trivial).
Helix strip: `et = {k: 0.22, W: 0.78, D: 4.1, elev: 4, fov: 39, s0: -4.75, s1: 5.8, fadeA: 1.3, fadeB: 1.5}`. Card pitch along the helix `ns = W * 0.8 + 0.026 = 0.65`. Card frame: tangent `(cos u * ax, -0.22, -sin u)` normalised as X, up as Y (so each card tilts along the pitch), normal X cross Y. ## 4. Gotchas and risks for implementers

1. Premotio's flight sim is heavy (a worker, 480-step integrations per card, a global air field). Do NOT port it. The look is reproduced by: golden-angle rails, tumble by slerp, spring bend and flutter, post smear. Verify against `f-00000..f-00450` by eye.
2. The smear is the signature. A plain RGB channel offset looks wrong (it reads as a cheap glitch). Implement the weight-ramp multi-tap, normalise by the weight sum, add the per-pixel grain offset.
3. The post pass needs the card render to have transparent background (premultiplied alpha). Mind colour space: the card render is stored in sRGB bytes and the pass works on them as is (they used `colorSpace = ''`, i.e. NoColorSpace, to keep the picture colours raw). Choose one convention and test with the paper background colour (premotio `#f5f5f7`; ours is the daybreak background token).
4. Pixel-matched camera: any resize must update `fov`, aspect, and the card base size (`Gp(frame)` clamp 0.55 to 1.15).
5. Ring and wall need orientation continuity: unwrap yaw (`dyaw` in Garuda) when slerping so cards never spin the long way; use `smootherstep` not linear for orientation.
6. Wall plane perspective: telephoto look needs a SEPARATE projection for the wall cards (their `lens: 2`, per-card `iLens` zoom/shift in clip space). Simplest equivalent: render the wall group with its own narrow-FOV camera into the same target after the hero cards, or place the wall plane far away (z about -1000) and scale it up 2x.
7. Depth sort: cards are transparent at edges. Use opaque cards with SDF discard + `alphaToCoverage` off and write depth, ordered by distance, or sort by view-space z each frame (20 to 40 cards: trivial).
8. Garuda's helix arc-length LUT matters only because of the 1.3 X stretch; for a circular helix (`ax = 1`) it is the identity, use that.
9. Scroll lock: Premotio has none. The owner says the user cannot scroll during the opening, handled by our existing lock; the hero card field can run its idle flow during the lock.
10. Text overlap in the hero: premotio hides letters that overlap card silhouettes; we do not copy this (the logo must stay clean).
11. Never copy the third-party images: both sites' pictures are theirs. Only our splash art is used.
12. Frame budget: premotio runs 1 draw for cards, 1 full-screen pass (16 taps max) + half-res pass + 160 px wake. At 1440x900 DPR 2 this is fine on integrated GPUs; if it is not, cheapen the pass for everyone (fewer taps, half-res reach), never gate by device.
