### 5.3 How the site plays a line today (where to hook the gain)

`apps/web/src/lib/audio.ts`: one Web Audio graph. `playVoice(url, {onEnd, volume})` (line 195): `loadBuffer(url)` (fetch with `cachedFetch`, decode), then `BufferSource -> GainNode(options.volume ?? 1) -> voiceBus (gain 1.9, a fixed +5.58 dB lift, "voice lines are recorded quieter than the music") -> analyser -> master -> DynamicsCompressor (threshold -14, ratio 3) -> destination`. The room's `features/library/voicelines/playback.ts` `toggleLine(url, onEnd)` calls `audio.playVoice(url, onEnd ? {onEnd} : {})`. The per-playback `GainNode` accepts any value, so a gain above 1 needs no engine change beyond documenting `volume` as linear and not 0 to 1 only. Full recordings play through the music element (`audio.playMusic`, volume clamped to 1 by `setMusicVolume`; the slot has a GainNode so a boost is possible).

### 5.4 Recommendation: gain in the data, applied at playback (no re-encode, no upload)

- Pipeline: add the measurement to `scripts/library/voicelines/build.py` (cache by file mtime, store `scripts/library/voicelines/loudness.json` or CSV) and write **`g` (number, dB, one decimal) on each `VoiceLine` and on `full`**; `VoiceLine` in `types.ts` gets `g?: number`. Never edit `voicelines-*.json` by hand. Playback: `volume = 10 ** (g / 20)` passed to `playVoice` from `toggleLine` and from the home section, which fixes `/collection/voicelines` and the home with one change.
- Formula, with the target at the output of the per-clip gain and the +5.58 dB bus lift kept (`BUS_DB = 20*log10(1.9) = 5.58`): **`g = min(TARGET - I - BUS_DB, CEIL - TP - BUS_DB)`**, where `I` is the clip's integrated LUFS, `TP` its true peak in dBTP, `TARGET = -18` LUFS and `CEIL = -1.0` dBTP. (If the bus lift is removed for library voice, drop `BUS_DB`.) Round to 0.1 dB.
- Why -18: music sits at -13 to -15 LUFS and the site ducks it during a voice (`duckMusic`), so speech at -18 reads clearly without the clipping risk of -14. Alternatives: -16 caps 1.4% of clips by peak, -20 caps none.
- Results on all 3,066 lines (computed): target -18 and ceiling -1 dBTP cap only **2 clips** by true peak (0.1%), 4 at -1.5; residual spread sd 0.01 LU. Without the bus the gain range is -3.9 to +29.5 dB (2,454 lines need more than +12 dB); with the bus kept, `g` is **-9.5 to +23.9 dB, median +9.8** (Japanese lines get little or a small cut, the quiet languages up to +24). The capped clips stay a little quieter than target; leave them. The existing master compressor (threshold -14, ratio 3) catches any stray peak. Optional: a brick-wall `DynamicsCompressor` on the voice bus (threshold -2, ratio 20, attack 0.003, release 0.1).
- Risk: boosting +20 dB lifts AAC coding noise and any room noise by the same amount. The clips are clean single sentences (LRA 0.5 to 3) and the originals are equally quiet, so a boost is the only way. Listen to the 10 quietest (Vexana es/pt/id 41, Selena pt 43, Lesley pt 2) before shipping.
- **Re-encode and re-upload alternative** (`ffmpeg -af "volume=<g>dB,alimiter=limit=0.89"` from the FLAC, AAC 96k, about 125 MB for the 3,092 files, new keys such as `voicelines/v2/...` because a key is never rewritten and R2 caches 30 days, then `r2.py sync` and a Cloudflare purge): heavier outward action on a shared bucket, changes `lineStem`, cannot be tuned later without another upload, and the downloads keep their original FLAC level anyway. Only worth it if a different player (a plain `<audio>`) must be level without Web Audio. Not recommended.
- Other numbers the home needs: the 10 characters for the chooser come from `voicelines.json.characters[]` (`slug, name, jp, romaji, aspirant, palette, splash "img/char/<slug>/splash-sm.webp"`, `lines`), cutouts are `img/char/<slug>/cutout.webp` (exists, e.g. fanny), splash `splash.webp`, `splash-sm.webp`, `extra-1.webp` (all in `public/img/char/<slug>/`). Language flags: `voicelines/flags.tsx` (SVG). Japanese first, then English, then the rest in `LANG_IDS` order (en, id, jp, ar, es, pt, tr, ru: reorder to jp, en, id, ar, es, pt, tr, ru for the home).

- **Fan art credits** (section 8): two items have no artist, one is not Aspirants specific. Group credits "Several artists" are legitimate.
- **Cache and keys**: never rewrite a key in R2; a changed file in `public/` under the same path needs the `media-store.ts` cache name bump. New pipeline outputs on D:, not C: (C: is nearly full; my own scratch here is small).
- **Repo size**: `public/video` is tracked and large already (4.2 GB on disk); keep new home video under about 25 MB in total and prefer reusing R2 keys.
- **Dashes**: copy and data written for the home must not contain em or en dashes (`common.write_json` and `scripts/qa/scan-text.mjs` enforce it). The Japanese text above has none, English "Chuckles" stage directions are in parentheses.
