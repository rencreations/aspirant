# Round three, H2-wall: the wall and the owner's words (20 Nov 2026)

Brief: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-home-feedback-3-request.md`, the wall part. The layout reference is the owner's premotio screenshot (the wall of big tilted pictures fills the left half with a hard edge, heading, one small paragraph and one button on the right).

## What changed

**Layout.** The wall's frame is the left half (`WALL_SHARE` 0.5 in `fx/image-field/wall.ts`, was 0.58; a tall screen keeps its top 56 percent). The cards are big: `GRID_SIZE` wide 0.25, tall 0.3 (`grid.ts`, was 0.15 and 0.18). At 1440 by 900: 74 cells, card 358 by 201 on the plane, pitch 401, about 7 rows cross the frame, about 11 slots have their centre inside it. The cut edge is hard (as before). `buildGrid(vp, share, sizes)` is the new pure builder (`wallGrid` memoizes the shipped numbers); the plane is shared with the rows, so **the rows' cards are big too** (H2-rows reads `wallGrid`). `inFrame` is now "the card's centre is inside the frame" (it had a 0.15 card margin).

**No background on the right.** `BACKDROP_AT.silk` is now `STORY.rows.start` (5.3), so the hero and the wall have no backdrop (plain daybreak ground). The silk begins with the rows. `backdrops.ts` and its test were edited for it.

**The words** (`sections/aspirants/`). One column, left aligned, in the right half: the title, ONE paragraph (`[game mark] original anime inspired skin series. A part of [TheMLabel mark] as the first featured series.`, flowing, two marks inline, `.paragraph` in `aspirants.module.css`) and the button. They are a TRIGGER (`words.ts`, pure): a clock of 0.9 s (`WORDS_SECONDS`, lines 0.14 s apart, each 0.62 s, cubic ease out) that runs forward when `wordsShown` says so and back 2.6 times faster otherwise. `wordsShown` has two sides per threshold: shown from the absolute scroll 2.9 (where the hero's zone ends, `WORDS_SHOW_AT` = `SLIDE_ORIGIN`), hidden 0.1 screens below it going back; hidden when the close has begun (`v` above 1.08) and back when `v` is 1.04 or less (the snap leaves the page a hair inside the zone, so the return threshold stands clear of `ZONE_EDGE`). The runtime keeps one number (the clock) and writes opacity and transform of three elements.

**The bug (words vanish while scrolling).** Found by reading the code, then checked with a real wheel down and up (log of every step in `D:\ren\aspirant-work\home\H2-wall\wordsbug.cjs`): in round two the words were a function of the section's story position driven by a play's clock and by the section's slot (`.slotText:not([data-on="1"]) { opacity: 0 }` cuts the whole text layer at the section's lead and tail, with no fade). Now the words have their own clock that no scroll position inside the rest can touch, and they only go at the two named thresholds, through a fade. The log shows opacity 1 on all three lines for every step of the rest (3.0 to 4.1 screens), a fade of about 0.3 screens when the close begins, and the words coming back on the way up at the rest. The slot's cut is still there: it happens 0.3 screens (the tail) after the words have gone.

**The wall follows the scroll.** `FieldScene.follow(scroll, viewport)` (`sections/hero/scene.ts`) calls `field.holdSlideAt(scrollSlide(geom, scroll))`; the aspirants runtime calls it every frame it runs (it runs in the hero's lead too, so the slide is exact at the landing and when the rows take over). The glide is gone from the scene (`slideRate` in `hero/timeline.ts` is unused now). `fx/image-field/scroll-slide.ts` (H2-rows's, shared) got `SLIDE_ORIGIN` 2.9: the plane stands still until the hero's zone ends and slides from there (`2 pitches per screen`, `SLIDE_PITCHES_PER_SCREEN`), so the cards land on a wall that does not move. `words.test.ts` keeps `SLIDE_ORIGIN` equal to `HERO_REST + HERO_ZONE`. Looked at with a real wheel: three offsets down and back to the start give the same frame, rows full at every offset.

**Spread and appear** (`formations.ts`). Every wall-only cell flies in from off screen along its own row, the way the row slides, between story p 0.5 and 0.94 (`EXTRA_WINDOW`, `EXTRA_BACK`: half a row's wrap length back, squared ease), the ones nearest the middle of the wall first, and fades in over the first tenth of the flight while it is still off screen. The cells outside the frame fly in too (the plane covers the screen while the cards land, then the clip closes on the frame, as premotio's does), so a cell that slides into the frame later was already there: nothing appears after p 0.94. The 18 hero cards still land from the ring. At 1440 the frame has about 11 slots, so some of the 18 land at its edge under the clip (the test says "inside the frame while there is room").

**No repeats near each other** (`arrange.ts`, pure, 51 tests). Deals the slots row by row: no picture twice in a row, two neighbouring rows share no picture, no Aspirant next to herself in a row (the wrap seam included) or beside the same Aspirant at about the same place in the row above, the same look-alike group (`WidePic.group`) kept apart the same way, a gap of `MIN_GAP` 8 places before a picture comes back, and when the pool is smaller than the plane the picture shown longest ago repeats. The rows slide in opposite directions, so across rows only "share nothing" holds at every offset; the Aspirant rule across rows is exact at slide 0 only. Wired through `buildFormations` (`poolKeys`, `poolGroups`).

**The pool.** 118 different pictures (the 18 splash arts and 100 of M-pool's `wall.json`, in its cyclic order, 640 wide files in `public/img/home/wall/`), more than the 74 cells at 1440, so every cell has its own picture at every size tested (1440, 1706, 1920, 390). `scripts/home/media/wall-tiles.cjs [COUNT]` writes `tiles-data.ts` and `preview/tile-pictures.ts` from the pool (do not edit them by hand). `WidePic` got `file` (a path under `public/`, used in place of the library key; no crop) and `group`. The preview opens all 118 (looked at: hover shows VIEW, a press opens "24 / 118", the arrow steps, Escape closes). The full size byte counts of the tiles in the preview list are estimates (`w * h * 0.075`). The waited list grew: `field:tiles` is now about 2.7 MB (was 0.74). M-pool's `pool.ts` (the async loader) and `pool-preload.ts` (first 24 waited, the rest deferred) are NOT used: the field's pictures are static data, so the pool is baked into `tiles-data.ts`. To use all 159, raise `COUNT` (GPU memory grows by about 1 MB a picture: a deferred, lazy decode would be the next step).

**The close** is a scroll zone (`aspirants/index.ts`): `ASPIRANTS_REST` 1 screen (words in, pictures slide with the scroll) then `ASPIRANTS_CLOSE` 1 screen, a zone from 1 to 2 (`aspirantsS`: the words go, the clip opens, the wall becomes the rows), idle snap 1.6 s for a full traverse (`ASPIRANTS_SNAP_SECONDS`). Section length 2 screens, lead = the hero's length, tail 0.3. The drone's stops (`drone-route.ts`, `["aspirants", 0.8 / 1.6, ...]`) still fall at local 0.5, which is the zone's start. Looked at with a real wheel (`snap.cjs`): a stop at 13 percent of the zone goes back to the rest and the words are back in; a stop at 40 percent is finished forward to the end (5.1); wheeling up 0.3 screens from the end goes back to the rest. The end state is the rows' first frame (the clip open, the plane at `scrollSlide(geom, 5.1)`).
