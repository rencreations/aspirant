# Home models: Silverbolt, Selena, the hand, the cameras (workstream M1)

Built 18 Nov 2026 by `scripts/home/models/` (README there: command, files, loader). Outputs in `apps/web/public/models/home/`: `silverbolt.glb` 300,108 B, `selena.glb` 1,753,200 B, `hand.glb` 271,760 B, `cameras.json` 13,949 B (4.2 KB gzipped), and a `<name>.facts.json` for each (also inside the GLB: `gltf.scene.userData.home`). Run `node scripts/home/models/build.mjs`: about one minute, byte for byte the same each time. Nothing here is in `apps/web/src`; the actors (SB, SL, HD) and the loading list (`PreloadItem`s, all `deferred`, by section) are still to write.

## Calls made while the owner slept

1. **Loader:** `new GLTFLoader().setMeshoptDecoder(MeshoptDecoder)` (`three/examples/jsm/libs/meshopt_decoder.module.js`). The files need `EXT_meshopt_compression`, `KHR_mesh_quantization` (required) and `EXT_texture_webp` (GLTFLoader reads it). No Draco, no KTX2. The site had no meshopt user before: the decoder is a new lazy import. All materials load as `MeshStandardMaterial` (the build removes `KHR_materials_specular`, which would give `MeshPhysicalMaterial`). `frustumCulled = false` on skinned meshes.
2. **No armature node, no Blender animation.** Bones hang on the scene root with plain armature-space transforms (glTF world = armature space = the space of `cameras.json`). The clips are baked from the evaluated pose and written by Node, one key a frame, (N - 1) / 24 s long. The entrance file's slotted actions make Blender's own animation export write clips that do not move (the recon's first prototype).
3. **Hair and skirt tracks stay** in Selena's clips (the choice the brief left open). They are the authored motion, and `interaction_03` has its hair whips at 0.4 s and 4.0 s that a spring only solve cannot match. `springs` in the facts lists every hair, skirt, ear, jewelry and cloth bone with its parent and rest length: a solver runs on top of the pose the mixer wrote (target = the animated pose), so hair follows the clip and also reacts to the cursor. Cost: about 0.5 MB.
4. **Selena's weapons:** the two white claws that float at her sides are inside `body_02` and `body_03` and show in the recording, so they stay. Her hands are empty in `interaction_03`. The abyssal form and the UI cards are gone.
5. **Silverbolt's tail is re-parented into a chain** in Blender, each bone re-oriented to aim at the next (local +Y along the tail, +X across, +Z up), the clips re-baked into chain-local tracks. Proof: every joint of every frame of every clip stays within 1.7e-5 m of the original rig; in Chrome the bone heads of the finished file are within 2.5 mm of Blender (3 frames per clip).
6. **Silverbolt's eyes.** The blue "gems" found by colour in the recon were wrong (that picks the violet horns). The eyes are the two almonds in the gold frames; they are found by their place on the atlas (glTF UV box `[0.115, 0.2, 0.18, 0.43]`, 14 triangles a side on the head bone) and cut into their own primitive, skinned to two new bones (`sb_eye_L`, `sb_eye_R`, at `(+-0.108, 3.576, 0.151)`). A flat silver socket 2.5 mm behind each is skinned to the head: when the eye bone is scaled in Y to 0.06 the socket shows as a shut lid. Drawn with culling on and a magenta ground: nothing shows through the head. The cheek crystals (UV box `[0.115, 0, 0.205, 0.23]`, 27 triangles a side on the cheek blades) are a fourth primitive with their own emissive material.
7. **The hand's cuff is open in the source** (a ring of 14 edges, not the "slit" the recon read). `holes_fill` would make a flat cap, so a sleeve is grown from the ring (0.16 m, tapering to half, five rings, a soft cone) and skinned to the forearm. The hand is turned so that the page needs no transform: wrist at the origin, forearm along +Z, palm up and toward the camera.
8. **The hand keeps the whole 1024 atlas.** The bracer's pieces lie over all of the body picture, so the crop the recon planned (u 0.007 to 0.569) is impossible with the bracer in. The recon was right that the normal map `n5x5` is not tiled (it is the same layout, half size).
9. **Facts are measured in three on the finished file**, not read off Blender: finger flexion axes and signs, eyeball forward axes, the jaw hinge, the blade axes, the tail pitch axis, the palm and where a thing laid on it sits. They agree with the recon (fingers flex about local +Z, the thumb about +Y on the left hand, -Y on the right).

## What was checked, and how

- **Rig maths:** the exporter's rest nodes against the rest transforms computed from the Blender matrices (below 1e-6); the re-parented tail against the source rig (1.7e-5 m); the tracks have no uneven scale (below 3e-5), so no shear.
- **A second opinion that does not come from this build.** The probes above come from the same evaluation that made the tracks, so a wrong evaluation would agree with itself (the slot hazard of the entrance file). The build therefore loads the owner's own `entrance.glb` (exported by Blender 5.1, whose keys start at 1/24 s) next to each finished file and compares every bone head at three frames of every clip (frame f of ours against its time (f + 1) / 24): Silverbolt worst 2.5 mm (`city_action` frame 0), Selena worst 0.8 mm; the limit is 6 mm. Each clip also has to move (`buildAnimations` stops a clip whose largest track spread is under 1e-3).
- **Clips:** bone heads at three frames of every clip, read back from the finished, compressed file in real Chrome, against Blender: Silverbolt 2.5 mm worst, Selena 0.84 mm, hand 0.13 mm. Durations are (N - 1) / 24 s. Loops end on their first key (the source's first and last keys differ by up to 3.5 mm in Silverbolt's root, and by up to 5.7 mm at a skirt bone head in Selena's `city_idle`; they are made equal). The digest's numbers at `interaction_03` 2.4 s (head, hand, `She_01`) agree to 6 mm (limit 12 mm).
- **Pictures looked at:** `silverbolt-angles.png` (front, 3/4, side, whole body, the same under `MeshBasicMaterial`; each shot at 1440 by 900 is also written alone), `silverbolt-head.png` (jaw open at 0.8, eyes shut, tail bent, and the open mouth with culling on), `silverbolt-clips.png`, `selena-faces.png`, `selena-blink.png`, `selena-clips.png`, `selena-material.png`, `hand-views.png`, `hand-closed.png`, `hand-closed-views.png`, `hand-breath.png`, `cameras-vs-recording-1..3.png` (12 moments). Small copies (WebP, 1.4 MB in all) are in `docs/design/home/models-selena-silverbolt/`; the full size PNGs, and each 1440 by 900 shot of Silverbolt alone, are in the work folder `D:\ren\aspirant-work\home\models\renders\`.
- **Camera against the recording:** 1.0 s to 3.2 s agrees (Selena's pose, framing, Silverbolt's head, the doll within 0.3 m). 0 to 0.3 s agrees. 0.4 to 0.9 s the recording lags the clip by about 0.2 s; after 3.4 s the recording keeps Silverbolt higher and farther away and after 4.4 s it lags by about 0.4 s. The owner wants those parts changed anyway.

## Tests

`node --test "scripts/home/models/**/*.test.mjs"` runs 16 tests of the pure code (`lib/rig.test.mjs`: matrices, rest world, the clip writer; `split.test.mjs`: cutting primitives; `facts.test.mjs`: bone and clip tables, spring rows, seams, the morph byte count). `pnpm check` does not run them (the repository has no test runner for `scripts/`); the Python helpers in `blender/` are covered by the asserts of the build (frame ranges, the weld, the open border count, the manifold checks, the rig proof).
