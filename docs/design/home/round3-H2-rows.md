# Round three, H2-rows: the rows and the wall to strip transition

The owner's feedback 3 (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-home-feedback-3-request.md`): the transition is scroll based, it must survive a stop in the middle, and the film that arrives must be as big as the pictures (it was small, so there was a size jump). Code: `sections/rows/`, small edits in `fx/video-plane/hero.ts`, `sections/backdrops.ts`, new `fx/image-field/scroll-slide.ts`.

## What changed

- **The rows follow the scroll.** No glide of their own, both directions: the plane slides `SLIDE_PITCHES_PER_SCREEN` (2) pitches for each screen of scroll (`fx/image-field/scroll-slide.ts`, `scrollSlide(geom, S)`, S the absolute scroll in screens). The runtime hands it to `field.holdSlideAt` each frame (`frame.S`). Looked at: the slide at 0.1 and 0.3 screens into the section is 2164 and 2260 px at 1440 (480 px a screen at the old 1.2, now 2), the same at the same scroll going back, and still after 1.5 s of idle.
- **The section is a zone** (K7, `kernel-scrub.md`). `zone.ts`: `ROWS_DWELL` 0.4 screens (the pictures only slide), `ROWS_ZONE` 1.5 (the transition, scrubbed), `ROWS_REST` 0.3 (the film stands upright as the strip while the strip's lead, 0.3, lies over it). 2.2 screens in all. `update(v)` gets the kernel's value: negative in the lead (the aspirants own the field), 0 in the dwell, 0 to 1 across the zone, 1 after it. If the visitor stops inside the zone the page finishes it (idle 220 ms, forward from 25 percent going down, back up to 75 percent going up). `ROWS_ZONE_SECONDS` 1.5 is the snap length for a full traverse.
- **Why the rest.** The strip runs in its lead with its rest value and draws the film at the strip's state (`strip/runtime.ts`, `Math.max(S, STORY.strip.start)`): a zone that ran into that lead would be overwritten by the strip half way (seen: the film jumped to the strip at 80 percent of the morph). Keep `ROWS_REST` at or above the strip's lead.
- **The beats** (`journey.ts`, story screens, `STORY.rows.length` 2.4): slide 0 to 1.1, the space (gap) 0 to 0.45, the film appears 0.45 to 0.8, the clearing 1.1 to 1.8, the morph 1.8 to 2.4. The film shows only once the space is open.
- **The film is a picture** (`fx/video-plane/hero.ts`): `grow = angleSize` (1, the same width as a card, 16 to 9, on the rows' plane with the rows' own tilt). It used to scale from 0.6, and the space was a card wide plus 0.2 of margin. Now the space is one more place of the row (`gapPush` = half a pitch: the neighbours slide apart by the row's own gap) and the film only fades in. Measured in the page (`rects.cjs`, the film and the field pinned by the lab, so the strip box shown before the film appears is the lab's, not the page's): film box 409 by 204 at rest in the rows, the cards in the middle of the screen averaging 388 by 185 (box of the tilted quad, same plane); then it grows and straightens to 504 by 136 with no jump: widths 414, 429, 451, 475, 494, 502, 504 and heights 213, 230, 234, 210, 170, 142, 136 at steps of 0.05 screens, centre moving 514 to 468 and 356 to 382. The proof that it never meets a picture is `clearing.test.ts` ("the film never touches a picture": from seven scroll starts, the film's rect against every card's, positive clearance the whole way).
- **The pictures clear along their rows** (`clearing.ts`): each card slides off along its own row, away from the film's column (1.8 card widths), and fades, the nearest first. Measured on the screen, not the plane (`visibleReach`: the farthest corner of the screen from the film, a step down counting 1.4 times a step across), because on the tilted plane an even front sweeps most of the visible cards in its first half. Front width 0.5 of the reach, linear.
- **The ride is planned from the scroll** (`slide.ts`, `planRide`): where the film starts, how far the rows slide and the slope of the slide's cubic come from the zone's start and the screen, never from what the rows were doing, so a seek, a jump and a scroll back are exact. The slide's cubic starts at the speed the scroll gave the rows (no jump where it takes over), `slope <= 2.5`.
- **Ground and backdrop** (`ground.ts`, `backdrops.ts`): the ground goes daybreak to storm over s 0.45 to 1.8 (`setGroundCue`, scroll linked, reversible). The silk of the wall fades out with it (`backdropAt` returns a `SILK_FADING` spec for S inside the rows' story, amount written in place). The ink field of the strip begins at `BACKDROP_AT.ink` = the end of the rows' story (S 7.7, the strip's start), where the ground is already dark and the silk is gone, so nothing flashes. Without this the strip's runtime, which runs in its lead, put the full silk back over the dark ground.
- Removed: `speed.ts` (the auto glide, the scroll boost), the clock play, `ROWS_PLAY_SECONDS`.

## Contract with the wall (H2-wall) and the strip (H3-strip)

- **The wall to rows joint is continuous whatever the wall does.** In the rows' lead (where the wall still owns the plane) the runtime notes `anchor = field.slideAt - scrollSlide(S)` every frame, and keeps the last one through the dwell and the zone (`slide = scrollSlide(S) + anchor`, and `planRide` takes it in `phase0`). So the rows take the plane over from a gliding wall or from a wall that follows `scrollSlide`, without a step. It is still better that the wall follows the same function (`field.holdSlideAt(scrollSlide(geom, S))`, S the absolute scroll in screens, `frame.S`, never the story axis): then the anchor is 0 and the whole page is one rule. The wall now does (`hero/scene.ts` calls `scrollSlide`; the function gained `SLIDE_ORIGIN` 2.9, the scroll where the hero's zone ends, so the plane stands still until the wall has landed). One rate for both: `SLIDE_PITCHES_PER_SCREEN` in `scroll-slide.ts`.
- The strip's first screen must equal the film's state at the end of the zone: `filmStateAt(STORY.strip.start, 0, ...)` (upright, 504 by 136 at 1440, overlay on). Both runtimes call it.
- The drone route is on the story axis and was not changed (`rows` stops: lower right in the dwell, round the film along the lower left, lower right again).
