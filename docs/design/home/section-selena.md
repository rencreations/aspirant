## 3. How it works

- **Everything is a function of `u`** plus the pointer. The room plays on the actors' own clocks (Selena `play("city_idle")` only when she is not in `city_idle` or `back_R` or when she was scrubbed; the dragon is a clip scrub by `frame.time`). The film is pure scrubbing: Selena `blend("city_idle", t, "city_intercation03", T, w)`, the dragon `clip(..., { root: "keep", blend })`, the camera from `cameraFor(T)`.
- **One world.** Silverbolt's `placeWorld` gets Selena's film-space position (object position plus the rotated minus `ANCHOR` times her scale), her scale and her quaternion, so both stand in the world of `cameras.json`. The doll is placed in world pixels with `place({ x, y, z, height: worldHeight x depthScale(z) })` (detached, pivot hips), so her world size is `0.26 m x Selena's scale` under any camera; she faces the camera by yaw.
- **The camera.** At `k` 0 the pixel camera, at 1 `cameraFor(T, vp, { draw: true })`. In between a camera of the section's own goes to `cast.setCamera`. The marks project through `cast.view()`, so they land right in both views. A phone asks for the lens `fit: "width"` with `maxFov` 112 so the doll and the dragon at the sides of the 16:9 frame still show (very wide; Selena's legs are large).
- **The drawer** is a gradient panel in the below layer (z 1, under the canvas), chosen over a pre-blurred still: the sky is a smooth gradient, so a blur of it shows nothing. It reads as frosted glass by a milky tint, the sky's lights drawn again large and soft inside it, a grain (a tiny SVG), a hairline edge. No `backdrop-filter`, no `filter`. The actors draw over it (the dragon may reach into it).
- **The white** is exactly the daybreak ground (`#f4f6f9` and the 28 px dot grid), so its hand-over and its leaving are invisible. Two layers: under the canvas (behind the dragon), over the canvas (to take Selena). Actors cannot be split across the DOM layers, so Selena is covered by white only after the dragon has left.
- **Marks.** While the section runs, pokes on the dragon become comic marks by the table of `fx-marks.md` (my own listener on `sb.onMark`, taken off on `leave`). The bite fires `flash` and `spark` at the `mouth` socket on a forward crossing of 3.5 s in a small step (never after a jump). The doll's release fires a `poof` at the hang point.
- **Interactivity flags** (`interactive` of Selena and Silverbolt, `setInteractive` of the doll) are asserted every frame: on in the room, off from u 2.0, on again at the end, on `leave()`. The actors' hit shapes project through the pixel camera, so they are wrong in the film.

## 7. Calls made without the owner

- **The film plays the baked dragon pass as the transition.** In the clip the dragon crosses the lens at 3.7 s and Selena flips after it (4.0 to 5.0 s). The white-out starts at the pass, so her flip is cut, and the dragon is not re-timed to pass later (the baked camera has moved on by then). The dragon keeps the clip's own dance; no procedural dance is mixed in.
- **The dragon is not drawn above the white.** The white behind it is the below layer, and Selena is covered by a white over the canvas after it has gone. Drawing the dragon in a pass of its own above a white quad inside the canvas would put it over the white from first to last; not built.
- **"Eats" is a flight to the mouth.** The dragon's head is on the other side of Selena from the doll in the baked clip, so the doll is flung across her to the mouth socket in half a second, with the jaw opening before. In the profile view the jaw gap is not clearly seen at the bite; the flash and the golden ticks mark it.
- **The doll's size and place** (0.26 m, over the `rightHover` socket) were matched by eye to the recording's frames (`cameras-vs-recording-*.webp`), not by its screen table.
- **Length unchanged (3.8).** The film is quick (about 4 seconds of clip for 0.85 screens). The integrator may give the section up to 4.75 screens: change `SELENA_SCREENS` and the table in `kernel/layout.test.ts` and `content`, and scale `AT` and `FILM_KEYS`.
- **The sky is ours:** the dusk gradient of the list, fixed stars, and one four point star at the left (the game's sky has it). It does not move with the baked camera (the sky is far).
