# Home: the rows and the strip (workstream S2)

The angled rows, the small film that turns upright, and the psychoactive hero: the strip with its words, the expand to the window with the play button, the pull into the orbit of the ten films under "Official animations for The Aspirants", and the fly-off to the bottom right. Request: "The Aspirants" (the scrolling part) and "Psychoactive hero" of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-home-page-request.md`; digest `docs/design/home-reference/ref-psychoactive-tib.md` (1.2 to 1.9, 2.3). Tools: the film plane (`fx-video-plane.md`), the picture field (`fx-image-field.md`), the drone (`actor-drone.md`, route in `drone-route.ts`). Code: `apps/web/src/features/home/sections/rows/` and `sections/strip/`.

## 1. The one rule: the film is a function of the absolute scroll

The rows (S 5.8 to 8.2, lead 0.5, tail 0.5) and the strip (S 8.2 to 11.3, lead 0.5, tail 1.2) overlap in S 7.7 to 8.7 and both drive the one `film` actor. So neither reads the order the kernel runs them in: `strip/film-state.ts` (`filmStateAt(S, travel, out)`) gives the film's whole state for any S, and both runtimes call it. `rows/journey.ts` and `strip/journey.ts` are pure modules of `s` (screens from the section's start); each runtime turns its `local` into `s` (`local * screens`), never `frame.S`, so a jump replays the right end. `ROWS_START`, `STRIP_START` and the lengths are constants checked against the registry by the tests (`journey.test.ts`); if the integrator moves a section, change them there. The drone is placed by `routeAt(S)` in both.

## 7. Numbers to tune

`rows/journey.ts`: `ROWS_BEATS` (slide, appear, clear, morph), `MIN_SLIDE_PITCHES` 2 (the rows slide at least this many pitches; the peak speed is 2.5 pitches a screen for a slide of 2 and 3.75 for 3). `rows/clearing.ts`: `ACROSS` 1.25 (the other rows count this much further from the film), `FEATHER_PITCHES` 1.1, `PUSH_WIDTHS` 1.5. `strip/journey.ts`: `STRIP_BEATS` (every beat of the strip), `TRAVEL_RATE` 2.1 rad a screen, the letters' `spread` (0.8 for the small words, 0.5 for the title, 0.9 for the lede), the exit of the words (first 0.3 of the fly-off). `strip/runtime.ts`: `SWAP_IN` 0.4 and `SWAP_OUT` 0.45 (the digest's), `NOISE_HZ` 22, `FOCUS_AT` and `FOCUS_RANGE` (where the page goes for a control that is not on screen), `DOT_OFF` (9 to 48 px), `PLUS_OFF` 14. `strip.module.css`: the type sizes are the digest's (`clamp(48px, 4.79vw, 92px)`, `clamp(52px, 6.4vw, 124px)`, mono 16 and 12), `.scrim` and `.ambScrim` (the dark behind the window's words and the second screen's words). Film: `focusX`, `focusY` (0.5, 0.45, VP's default, not changed: the strip shows the two characters).

## 9. Calls made without the owner

- **The ground goes dark in the rows** (`bg` storm, nav theme storm), at the joint with the aspirants (5.55 to 6.05), so J3 is dark and the kernel's cross-fade does it. The brief said the section's bg is the dark ground.
- **The rows slide in opposite directions** (the field's own: the even rows one way, the odd the other), not all to the left: the joint with the aspirants is exact and the middle row, which carries the film, goes left. Making every row go left is a change in the field (`rowWay`).
- **The other cards leave by a clearing around the film**, not by scrolling off the left: a wrapping row cannot empty itself by sliding.
- **The film's gap**: it rides a gap of the middle row, found by arithmetic from the rows' geometry (not by `slotRect`): the slide is a whole number of pitches so the gap ends at the middle.
- **The small state has no "PLAY REEL" and no duration on the line** (the owner removed play there). The window has "PLAY FILMS" and "03:00". The reference's "SINCE" and "N DAYS" corner labels, the confetti and balloons, the mist and the CRT hologram are not built.
- **The duration is "03:00"**: the trailer is 180.8 s and the Films room writes whole seconds (`clock`), as the player does (0:00 / 3:00). `fx/video-plane/data.ts` has `durLabel` "03:01" (rounded); VP's, not changed.
- **The fly-off starts at S 10.9 and ends at 11.9**, so J4 (S 11.3) is mid-flight, not finished: the design's J4 reads "leave to the bottom right" and Silverbolt enters at 11.46. The tail is 1.2 screens for it.
- **Letters come in at random moments scrubbed by the scroll** (the reference's SplitText), in the server markup (`Chars`: the plain text for a reader and the letters, aria-hidden, for the eye), so nothing rewrites React's DOM.
- **A control that is not on screen** (keyboard focus) takes the page to where it shows (small frame, window, orbit) and stops the shell's own jump to the middle of the section.
- **The bar's three dots (the reference's scroll button)** is kept (a real button), though the site has no scroll cues in its joins: it is the reference's own part of the hero.
- Colours and type follow the site (Big Shoulders for the big words, JetBrains Mono for labels), not the reference's Roobert; the pill is the site's.
