# Round four: the bolt and the fall (SEC-bolt-fall, 20 Nov 2026)

The owner's message: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-home-feedback-4-request.md`. Code: `sections/bolt/` and `sections/fall/` (the two draw one scene), small edits in `sections/hand/` (backdrop plan, J6) and `content.ts` (the words). No CI was run (the owner's process rule): one vitest file for the pure journey (and one quick run of the touched tests), `tsc` filtered to these paths, and frames read by eye.

## What changed

- **Plain scroll, no snap, no wait.** Both sections lost their plays and zones (`index.ts` has none). `update` gets the scroll's own `local`; the runtimes turn it into `u`, the scroll in screens since the bolt's start (`bolt/stations.ts`: `BOLT_SCREENS` 3.7, `FALL_SCREENS` 4.5, bolt lead 0.5, fall lead 0.5, fall tail 0.3). A section without plays blocks the kernel's joint snap over its whole range, so nothing near them snaps (checked with a real wheel: idle drift 0.001 to 0.006 screens after a stop in the bolt, none in the fall). The hand's own zone snap and the strip's exit zone snap are unchanged.
- **Blue through the end.** `bolt/backdrop-plan.ts` asks for the strip's own `ink-field` (storm, the same colours, no other kind ever). Its `amount` fades from 0.9 to 0 over the bolt (`backdropAmount`); at 0 the section stops calling `set` and the backdrop costs nothing. No aurora, lightning or speed lines remain. `bolt/lines.ts` colours are two blues (the constants keep their old names `WHITE` and `MINT`, for the test).
- **White from the bottom (tibdigital "Since 2007").** A 1.35 screens tall off white gradient layer (`.white` in `bolt.module.css`, `#f3f1ec`, a long soft top edge) lifted by `translate3d(0, y%, 0)` only. `whiteRise(u)` in `journey.ts`: starts at u 0.35, 0.68 at the shake, 0.74 through the fall's move, back down in the fall's last half screen (the hand rises over a dark ground). A section's DOM slot is only on while the section runs, so there are two copies (`data-bolt-white="bolt"` in the bolt's slot, `"fall"` in the fall's); the scene writes both and in the overlap (the fall's lead) the fall's copy shows it.
- **Text wheel exactly as tibdigital** (`bolt/wheel.ts`, numbers from `ref-psychoactive-tib.md` 2.5): 5vw (72 px at 1440), weight 400, tracking -0.06em, perspective 1000, `translateY(r*90px) translateZ(-100|r|px) scale(1-0.15|r|) rotateX(-25r deg)`, opacity `max(0,1-0.6|r|)` times the grey `(102 + 153 max(0,1-1.5|r|))/255`. The wheel position `wheelAt(u)` is continuous, 0 to 4 over u 1.45 to 3.45 (no stations). Each name is drawn twice (white and ink copy) and cross-fades by opacity when the white's solid part reaches the middle of the screen (`inkShare`: rise 0.37 to 0.46). Both copies carry a still soft halo so the line reads over the dragon's pale head. The real DOM list is kept; the `h2` stays screen reader only.
- **The dragon.** `journey.ts` places the HEAD: a straight diagonal along 0.85 of the screen's diagonal angle, from beyond the lower right to beyond the upper left. `headSpeed(u)` is 1 outside the slow range (u 1.3 to 3.7) and `SLOW_SPEED` 0.12 in its middle, smoothstep ramps of 0.55 screens at each end; `pathParam` integrates it (a table), so the middle of the range is the middle of the path and the head crawls about a fifth of the screen's width across the whole range. `scene.ts` puts `headPoint` on that point by reading last frame's offset between the body's place and the head (so lean and shake do not move the head). Side view through `place({ heading })`, `height = length / SilverboltActor.LENGTH_M`, length `0.6 * max(w, 0.9h)`. The pointer only leans the body (`sb.follow(1)`, as SB-dragon asks).
- **The shake and the fall.** From u 3.25 to 3.95 the scene keeps `sb.shake(1)` going (it fires again whenever the envelope drops under 0.3, so a visitor who lingers sees a continuous rattle). At u 3.7 (`BOLT_SCREENS`, crossing forward) it calls `doll.loseGrip()`; `doll.onLetGo` releases her, and from then on she is placed in the screen (`doll.fallLoop(1)`, DOLL-fall's own clock). A jump past the throw releases her at once with no seam. A scroll back above 3.7 seats her again (`rideOn`).
- **The fall's position and camera.** `Journey` (pure of `u`): her screen place goes from the seat to the middle of the screen, her height from the riding height to `END_HEIGHT` (0.38 of the viewport height), the zoom with the same `easeInOut` (a cubic), over `FALL_AT.move` (1.5 screens). The camera (`camX`, `camY`, `zoom`) is solved so her world fall (0.9 of the height, eased) lands her on that place; the dragon uses the same camera, so it is left behind and pushed out. Nothing drives the fall clip from the scroll.
- **The words.** `content.ts`: FIVE | PHASES and TEN | HEROES (test updated). They come in over 0.65 screens after the move has ended, FIVE | PHASES stand to 2.9, turn into TEN | HEROES (2.9 to 3.55), and leave upward in the hand's lead (4.05 to 4.4). Left word centred in the left half (`grid 1fr 1fr`, `justify-self: center`), right word in the right half, all at the middle of the height (`align-items: center`, no padding), ink on the white, FIVE and TEN in the accent. Phone: above and below the doll.
- **No effects.** The scene has no marks, trails, air, speed lines, bangs, clouds or backdrop kinds. Only the dragon, the doll, the blue lines (until the shake), the white, the text.
- **The hand's joint (J6).** She ends at the middle of the screen now: `hand/timeline.ts` `J6` is feet `-(0.396/1.7) * 0.38`, height 0.38, `airY` 0, `airHeight` 0.38 (the test follows `END_HEIGHT` and `END_FEET_Y` of `bolt/journey.ts`). The hand's backdrop (`hand/backdrop-plan.ts`) starts from nothing and brings the galaxy in from beat 0.15 (the old plan continued the fall's aurora); `hand/runtime.ts` skips `set` while `plan.on` is false (one line).
- **Tests.** Deleted the old `journey`, `stations`, `wheel` and `backdrop-plan` tests of the bolt (their subjects were rewritten). New `bolt/journey.test.ts` (11 tests: the slow range, the middle of the path, the wheel, the white, the blue, the fall's camera). `hand/backdrop-plan.test.ts`, `hand/timeline.test.ts` and `content.test.ts` follow the new numbers.

## Not done, not verified

- The strip's "See Collections" button was gone at every position I shot, so I could not reproduce the owner's report that it stays over the bolt (it fades out with the letters of the strip's exit, gone by about u -0.6).
- The text at the moment of the cross-fade over the dragon's head is the least legible frame (the halo helps); the cross-fade is a fixed range of `rise`, not measured on the picture.
- The doll's riding pose and the hand's landing blend are the other helpers'; the seat and grips come from SB-dragon's sockets (`seat`, `gripL`, `gripR`, `headPoint`) and `doll.rideOn`, called through a type that tolerates a missing method (`Rider` in `scene.ts`; delete that cast once the API is final).
- No e2e, no production build, no perf pass. A real phone, Firefox and Safari were not tried. The dragon's offset servo reads the last frame's matrices: a very hard flick can show the head one frame off.
- Cursor labels on the dragon (FLAP, POKE) come from the actor's hit targets and are unchanged.
