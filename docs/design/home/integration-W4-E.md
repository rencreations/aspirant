# Home, wave 4 integration: tests, the plain page, accessibility, the loading list (W4-E)

Range: `e2e/home.spec.ts`, the other specs that touched the old home, the unit tests and knip, the home as a document (headings, landmarks, focus, contrast), the loading list, the ways into the page (reload, Back, intro off, no scripts). No visuals.

## What changed

- **`e2e/home.spec.ts` is rewritten for the real home.** It no longer expects the stub actors, never waits for `networkidle` (the home downloads in the background: it waits for `[data-home][data-open="1"]`, `homeOpen` in `e2e/helpers.ts`, or for `window.__home`). Groups: the document (one h1, headings in the page's order with no level skipped, every control and link named, the copy of the owner exact, the ten links of the list in order, the takedown links), the stage (one canvas, hidden from a reader; the scene follows the scroll in the middle of each of the fifteen fixed sections and shows only its own words; axe at the middle of all sixteen sections; a hidden control never catches a press; focus on a control moves the page; the first and last control of ten sections show when focused from far away; the whole tab order is in page order, named, with a focus mark), the plain page without WebGL (readable, headings in order, no sideways scroll, axe, the keyboard reaches every control and each shows, every link answers), the server's page without scripts (one h1, the words, the links, the boot cover hidden, no sideways scroll), the ways in (the opening off: open at once; the opening on: tap gate, inert page, a press, Enter; Back from About opens the home at rest with no second opening; a reload part way down opens at the top) and the ways out (button, N, scroll to the end, not by scrolling most of the way). Runs with `E2E_BASE_URL=http://localhost:3100/ npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts` (the config's own static server needs `pnpm build`, which helpers do not run).
- **`e2e/shell.spec.ts`**: two tests waited for `networkidle` on `/` and one clicked "Continue to About" before the home was open. They use `homeOpen`. `a11y.spec.ts`, `smoke.spec.ts` and `menu.spec.ts` pass against the new home unchanged (desktop project, dev server).
- **`kernel/focus.ts` (new, tested)** and a `data-home-focus-at="<screens from the section's start>"` attribute: a control may say where it shows. `home-stage.tsx` uses it, then the section's own `focus` fraction, then the middle. Used by the strip (its "See All" shows only in the first 0.5 screen, the play button at 1.35, the last button and the ten film links at 2.4) and by the ten archive covers (`focusAt(i)` in `archive/view.tsx`: the rest of the drone over the cover, never before the cover has risen). Before this, Tab from far away put the page in the middle of a section where the control was invisible (strip) or off the screen (first archive cover).
- **Plain page (no WebGL, no scripts)**: the strip's scrim is 2.7 times wider than the screen and made the page 2088 px wide at 1440 and 566 at 390: `.root` is now `overflow-x: clip` in plain mode (`home.module.css`). Selena's words (title, line, button) were `opacity: 0` in the plain page with scripts and no WebGL, and absolutely placed: they show now, in the flow (`selena.module.css`). The list's character names are links 22 px tall: 28 px minimum in the plain page (axe `target-size`).
- **Loading list**: the plain `img` of the list (ten posters) and of the archive (ten covers from the bucket) had the default priority and competed with the drone's files (about 1.6 MB in the document, found by a request log); they are `fetchPriority="low"`. `preload-items.test.ts` (new) holds the list as a contract: every id once, the waited items are an explicit allow-list (a new waited item has to be added to the test on purpose), the waited local files stay under 4 MB, every deferred item has a priority, the drone's chunk leads on a document load, Silverbolt, the doll, the hand, Selena, the gramophone, the previews and the voice lines are deferred.

## Measured

- Real Chrome, 1440x900, dev server: a real-wheel walk of all 60 screens: no page error, three warnings (an unused preload of a CSS chunk, two harmless shader precision notes).
- axe (wcag2a, 2aa, 21a, 21aa, 22aa, best-practice) at 21 places of the scroll at 1440x900 and 12 at 390x844: no violation of any impact in stage mode. In plain mode one (`target-size`, fixed).
- Text contrast of every text node against its theme's flat ground: two hits under 4.5, both false: the strip's "See Collections" (a light filled pill, dark text on `rgb(238 243 250)`) and the footer's "About" (white text on the star button's dark chamfered fill, drawn by pseudo-elements, which the script does not see; the picture shows it). axe's `color-contrast` was clean at every place. Text over pictures, the film and the canvas cannot be computed by a script: not checked.
- Tab: 116 stops in stage mode in page order, all with an outline, all named, all visible once the section has settled (about one second). The 80 voice "Play" buttons are in the selected panel only (roving tabs). The bend section has 32 stops (a credit link and a takedown link for each of 15 pictures); the fan art credits are the owner's rule, so it is left.
- Waited bytes: the production build was not made (helpers do not run `pnpm build`), so there is no measured production figure. A request log of the dev server cannot separate waited from deferred files (the localhost link is so fast that everything is in before the gate). By the sizes of the files in the waited list: the strip's 1280 clip 2.3 MB, the 18 wide cards 2.4 MB, the ten portrait cards 1.2 MB, the drone 0.1 MB, its faces, the two logos and the chunks (about 1 MB of chunks in production): an estimate of about 5.5 MB, which is what the design says. `preload-items.test.ts` holds the local files of the waited list under 4 MB.

## The `data-home-focus-at` contract (for every section)

A control that shows itself only at one place of its section writes `data-home-focus-at="<screens from the section's start>"` (a number from 0 to the section's length). When the keyboard reaches that control while its section is off, the page goes there (`kernel/focus.ts`, `focusScreens`), not to the middle. Order: the control's attribute, the section's own `focus` (a fraction of its length, `HomeSection.focus`), the middle. A section that is already running moves the page itself, as the list, the strip and the archive do. The strip and the archive covers use it now; a new section whose controls are hidden in the middle should too, and `e2e/home.spec.ts` ("every section's first and last control shows when the keyboard reaches it") is the test to add its section to.
