# Home: the archive, the footer and the way to About (workstream S7b)

The last two scenes of the home (`/`, screens 56.1 to 60.3): the ten rooms of the collection as ten covers with the drone as the spotlight, then the redesigned footer, and the scroll's way out to `/about`, where the home drone becomes About's opening drone. Request: "Closing" of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-home-page-request.md` (last paragraph). Code: `apps/web/src/features/home/sections/archive/`, `sections/footer/`.

Interactions. Hover (mouse, pen) or keyboard focus on a cover pulls the light and the drone to it (the drone's face becomes "sparkle", the site's cursor says OPEN); the pull lets go 0.35 s after the pointer leaves, so the gap between two covers is no flicker. A key focus also scrolls the row to the cover. On touch, the first tap on a cover the light is not on pulls the drone to it and scrolls the row to it (it does not open), the second tap on the lit cover opens the room. All ten covers and "Open the collection" are real links (the whole cover is the link, "Open the room" is its words, no nested link, one tab stop each). The drone itself is the actor's own button (hover, press answers).

The footer: the owner's line "Scroll more to see the about page", a ring where the drone rests, the title "About", a visible button "About" (accessible name "Continue to About", key N), the hint "Keep scrolling or press N", and the foot every page of the site carries: Made by MLBB Community, Terms, Privacy, Cookie settings (the menu's own button), "Copyright or takedown" (a `ReportLink` naming the home's 3D models and fan art: design call 10), and the fan-project notice (`NOTICE` of `features/about/content.ts`, imported, not copied).

## 2. How it is built

```
sections/archive/geometry.ts   pure: the strip (cover size, pitch, where the drone hovers, the slide share k), the tour (rests and glides), lit, nearest
sections/archive/route.ts      pure: every time (AT), the drone's route from J14 to the exit, Tetra's goodbye, the covers' rise and lift, the light, the title, the hairline, the footer's stage top, About's entry point
sections/archive/runtime.ts    the archive: DOM writes, hover and focus and tap, the beam and the pool, the drone and Tetra up to 58.6
sections/archive/view.tsx      server: title, ten links with covers and counts, the button, the beam and the pool (below layer)
sections/archive/archive.module.css, preload.ts (+ tests beside)
sections/footer/runtime.ts     the footer: hairline, the pool under the ring, the drone from 58.6 (J15 and the exit), the exit hook's dart
sections/footer/view.tsx, footer.module.css, cookie-button.tsx, exit-hook.tsx, exit-bridge.ts, preload.ts
footer-action.tsx              additive: classes of the card passed in (actionsClassName, hintClassName), the button has its arrow
kernel/handoff.ts              two small edits, see 4
content.ts                     ARCHIVE (rooms, items, open) and FOOTER (button "About", legal block)
scripts/qa/probes/home-s7b.cjs the probe, see 6
```

**One function places the drone.** `droneAt(S, raw, q, vp, strip, foot, out)` in `route.ts`. The archive's runtime writes the drone for 56.1 to 58.6 and the footer's runtime from 58.6 (its lead), each with the same function, so the order they run in cannot matter. Below 56.1 the archive writes neither the drone nor Tetra (the bend section's tail runs to 56.6 and writes them), and from 56.1 it owns both. `AT.start`, `AT.stuck`, `AT.end` and `AT.handover` are held equal to the registry by a test (`route.test.ts`), so a change of any section length fails a test instead of drifting.

**Screen frame in, stage frame out.** The route works in pixels from the top left (the DOM's frame); `toStage` converts once, where the actors are placed.

**The footer card has no fill.** The kernel's sticky stage (`.flowStage`) is opaque and sits over the canvas, which would hide the drone. `footer.module.css` clears its fill, shadow and radius for `section[data-home-section="footer"]` (specificity one element above the kernel's rule, no edit of `home.module.css`) and draws the card as a hairline frame with a soft lip. The fixed daybreak ground shows through; the drone is drawn under the text layer and stands in the ring. The card's own rise is the page's native scroll. Everything glued to it (the ring, the pool) uses `frame.raw` (the real scroll), not the scene's smoothed `S`: `stageTop(raw) = max(0, 59.1 - raw) * H`, plus the ring's offset in the stage, measured on resize (`getBoundingClientRect` of the ring against the stage's, a difference that does not depend on the scroll).

**Writes.** Transform and opacity only. Per frame: one transform for the row, beam (2 values), pool (2 values), and a cover's custom properties (`--in`, `--out`, `--lit` on its list item, in steps of 0.02, written only when they change; CSS turns them into transform and opacity). Nothing on `<html>` or `<body>`. Nothing is allocated per frame in the pure code (every function writes into an `out`); the strings of the DOM writes are made only when a value changed (the row's transform and the beam change every frame while the tour moves: three template strings).

## 3. Loading (`preload.ts` of both)

All deferred. Archive: its chunk (`home-archive`, priority 70), the ten covers of the shelf from the R2 bucket (`coverItem`: `fetchWhole` with the bytes counted, then a decode with `crossOrigin="anonymous"`, the way the page's `img` asks; priority 71; 35 to 147 KB each, about 0.8 MB), the drone and Tetra lists (shared with the bend section: an item two lists share is kept once). A cover reached before it has arrived shows the room's colour (its frame's background). The counts come from `public/data/library/index.json` imported in `view.tsx` (the About page does the same), so they are in the server HTML and need no loading. Footer: its chunk (priority 74) and the drone. The card, links and button are the page's HTML.

## 4. The way to About, and what was changed outside the folders

`kernel/handoff.ts`, two small edits (re-read before each, listed here for the integrator):

1. **About's opening was skipped.** The change used to push `/about` while the page was still scrolled to the end of the home. About's drone director (`features/about/drone/director.ts`, read only) starts its flight only if `window.scrollY <= 8` when its scene is ready, which comes before `whenPageReady` and the scroll to the top. So the flight never played (phase went `wait` to `done`). Now the change scrolls to 0 (Lenis immediate and `window.scrollTo`) while the cover hides the page, before the push. Verified: About's phases read `wait, title, landing, landed, done` after the handoff.
2. **The exit hook runs before the cover, not with it.** The cover is mostly opaque after 150 ms, so run together the drone's flight was never seen. Now `await exitHook()` then `await cover.show()`. For the scroll's way in the hook has nothing to wait for (the drone left during the scroll, `GONE_AT = end - 0.06`), so there is no delay; for the button and the N key from the footer it adds the 450 ms dart. A drone not on the footer, a runtime not mounted (any other scroll position, the plain page) and a hidden tab (1.4 s safety) all resolve, so the page always changes.

**The exit hook lives in two parts** because `kernel/handoff.ts` is a client module (hooks) and the footer's runtime is a lazy chunk that the server's module graph also walks, where importing it fails the build. `exit-hook.tsx` ("use client", rendered in the footer's markup) calls `setExitHook`; the runtime puts its exit in `exitBridge.run` (a plain module) while it is mounted. `exit-hook.test.tsx` holds: set while the footer is on the page, resolves at once when the runtime is not there, waits for the runtime's own exit.

**The first About frame.** About's pass starts at `x = vw + 0.75 size` and `y = title.y + 0.4 title.h` with `size = passSize(title)`. The home drone ends past the right edge at that height and size. The title's box is read from the live page at five sizes (1440x900: h 388.8, y 80; 1706x950: 441.2; 1366x768: 356.7; 390x844: 241.8, y 64; 820x1180: 365.8) and `aboutEntry` is a fit (landscape: `y = size = min(0.27 H, 0.36 W)`; portrait: `y = 0.186 H`, `size = min(0.36 W, 0.185 H)`), good to about 6 px at those sizes. If the About page's title changes, run the probe's handoff part and compare, then adjust `aboutEntry` (`features/about` is read only here). Home `height` and About `size` differ by `SIZE_PER_HEIGHT` (1.385).

## 8. Calls made without the owner

- **The card is transparent** (section 2): the drone must be seen on it, so the card is a hairline frame, not a filled plate.
- **Tetra says goodbye and runs off** at the start. The request does not mention her after the bend section; J14 has her at rest beside the drone, so she waves and leaves instead of popping out.
- **Covers in one row**, not a grid: the shelf's five by two grid does not move with the scroll and the owner asked for a calm movement; one row lets the drone fly from card to card and the row slide under it. The covers are the shelf's own (the same files and order).
- **Counts** are the library index at build time (the About page does the same).
- **The takedown shortcut** is the footer's (design call 10 says the archive or the footer). The archive carries none.
- **The button reads "About"** (the owner: "a visible About button"); its accessible name stays "Continue to About" (the e2e and the plain page use it).
- **The exit hook runs before the cover** and the scroll goes to the top before the push (section 4). Both are kernel edits.
- **Layout length** of both sections is unchanged (3.0 and 1.2 screens, lead 0.5 and 0.5, tail 0.5 and 0): the registry test holds them.

## 9. Not done, not verified

- Not run: the production build, `scripts/perf/weak.mjs`, Firefox, Safari, a real phone (a touch context in desktop Chrome was used), a slow connection (the ten covers were never watched going through the bar), a context lost for good during the archive.
- Not seen in a running page: the "Cookie settings" button opening the consent banner (it calls `useConsent.reopen`, the menu's own call), and the takedown link's page.
- The drone's "bow" and the heart bubble at the footer are the actor's pose; the pose's timing against a slow scroll was not tuned.
- The About entry fit (`aboutEntry`) is a copy of numbers from a read-only module, good to about 6 px at five sizes; a different viewport (an ultrawide, a very short window) was not measured. About's opening is also skipped for any page that is scrolled down when its drone is ready, so a more robust fix belongs in About's director (read the scroll after `REVEAL_EVENT`); the kernel edit above avoids the need.
- The comic marks of the bend section fire again (a poof cloud and stars at the paper's place) when a probe seeks past it into the archive, because the bend's runtime replays its end as a passed section (its `popped` flag is not set). It does not happen when scrolling down from the bend. Not mine, not fixed.
- Beyond my folders: in the plain page (no WebGL) the `strip` section's `.ambScrim` makes the document 648 px too wide (`e2e/home.spec.ts` "lays the sections out as a readable document" fails on that, not on the archive or the footer). The e2e specs that `waitForLoadState("networkidle")` on `/` time out because the home downloads in the background (the kernel's notes say the same).

The times in section 1 were moved by W4-D (the first cover no longer rises where Tetra stands); the numbers in `route.ts` (`AT`) are the truth. See `integration-W4-D.md`.
