# Home section: bend (workstream S7a)

Tetra closes the book, stands, and bends the fan art of The Aspirants out of the edges of the screen into garuda2026.offwg.com's hero (posters at different tilts round her), then into a 3D circle, throws the circle away, calls a spiral of the ten splash arts round her whose last picture is the drone's paper, and the drone climbs out of the paper, pats her head, hugs her and goes to the rest beside her. Nine screens (47.1 to 56.1), lead 0.5, tail 0.5, ground daybreak. Code: `apps/web/src/features/home/sections/bend/`. Brief: the owner's paragraphs "Bending and fan art" (`2026-11-18-home-page-request.md`), joints J13 and J14 (design 4.1).

## Why it has its own card layer and not `fx/image-field`

The field is one pass at order 12, always behind Tetra (27): a ring or a spiral could not pass in front of her. It has 18 cards with its own ten portraits and eighteen wide pictures, and this section needs 22 posters of fifteen other pictures plus eleven spiral cards. So the section draws its own cards (`cards.ts`, `card-shader.ts`): two shared passes of the stage, `bend-back` at 24 (under Tetra, 27) and `bend-front` at 28 (over her, under the cast at 30 and the drone's front layer at 35). A card with z above 0 is in the front pass. The shader is the field's (raw sRGB, rounded corner by distance, a hair of rim shade, soft shadow, streak-free) plus an out of focus (a ring of eight taps at a mip level that grows with the blur, and a softer rim) and a paper mode for the drone's card. The field is not touched and is not shown here.

## What the visitor can do

- **Tetra**: her own targets (hover "HI", presses answer with hops, laughs, a startle, annoyed from the fourth). The cards' targets have a smaller depth where they are in front of her, so a card in front wins and one behind her loses.
- **The fan posters and the ring**: the hover lifts the poster 46 px toward the camera, 5 percent bigger, sharp (the blur gives way), a deeper shadow, neighbours part by the flow of the field's rule. The site's cursor says `CREDIT`. A credit plate stands beside the card (the real DOM, `data-bend-plate`): the "Fan work" badge, the title, the credit line from the data (`CreditLine`), "View the original", and `ReportLink` ("Copyright or takedown", with the picture named). A press pins the plate (so its links can be pressed); a press on nothing lets it go. A touch tap does the same. The plate never stands under the top bar (`NAV_INSET`).
- **The splash cards** lift on hover (no plate: they are official art).
- **The drone**: its own targets, all the way from the paper. At the rest it follows the pointer politely (a tenth of the way, at most 6 percent of the width and 5 percent of the height); touch gives no offset.
- **Keyboard**: the DOM order is the scroll order (the fan title and its button, the takedown link, the fifteen credit plates, the artwork title and its button). A group that is off is `opacity: 0` and `pointer-events: none`, never `visibility: hidden`, so the keyboard reaches it. Focus on a control of a group that is not on the screen moves the page (`scroll.to`, immediate) to the beat where it shows (`FOCUS_BEATS` in `runtime.ts`); a focus that arrived before the section was built is answered when it has (the stage moves the page to the section's middle first). Checked with a real Tab: the first press ends at 2.9 screens in with the group fully on and the pill showing its focus ring.

## Pictures and the takedown rules

- The fan pictures are the 15 credited group pictures of The Aspirants picked by the media workstream (`public/data/home/picks.json`, `fanart.items`; `fan-data.ts` is a small copy and `pictures.test.ts` holds it equal, canon and credit included). Shown in a random order for each visit (a seed from `Date.now()` and `Math.random()`, `order.ts`): the first 15 posters of the wall each have a picture of their own, the others repeat pictures that are not on the next poster.
- Everything about the badge and the takedown follows `canon` of the record (`canon !== "official"`): `CanonBadge`, `CreditLine`, `ReportLink` on each plate and the one section link ("Fan art on the home page"). Nothing is keyed to a name. The credit line and the link to the post are the collection's own `CreditLine`.
- The ten splash cards are `HERO_PORTRAITS` of the field's `picks-data` (official art only), in the order of the carousel. The drone's picture is `/img/about/drone.webp` cut to the drone (`DRONE_CROP`), decoded with straight alpha multiplied in.

## Loading (`preload.ts`)

All deferred: the section is the fourteenth screen of the story. In the order of the visitor's arrival: the chunk `home-bend` (62), Tetra (`tetraItems({ kite: false, priority: 60 })`), the drone (`droneItems({ priority: 10 })`), the marks, the 15 fan pictures (64, 2.68 MB, from the library, CORS), the ten splash cards (66, the hero's own files, so the browser already has them) and the drone's still (66, 36 KB). Pictures are fetched whole through `fetchWhole` (real bytes, a stall or a drop retried) and decoded with `createImageBitmap` at the size the budget allows (`bitmaps.ts`, not `imageItem`: a non-CORS `Image` poisons the CORS read WebGL needs). A bitmap the list has already made is taken from the store; a picture that is reached before it has arrived shows the card's own colour (the picture's mean) and comes up out of it when it does. No `expectReady`: nothing here is seen at first paint.

**GPU memory** (mips included): the 15 fan pictures at 704 on the long side, 34.4 MB; ten splash cards at 704 by 880, 33 MB; the drone's still, 1.4 MB. The fan pictures go to the GPU when the section is built (two screens before it) and go back at 6.15 screens; the splash cards go at 2.5 screens and stay to the end. At most about 69 MB at once (2.5 to 6.15 screens). The bitmaps are closed after their first upload. The picture field, if it is alive, holds its own copy of the same ten portraits (61 MB at 960 by 1200): not shared, the two live in different sections. Uploads are one a frame.

## Calls I made without the owner

- **Own card layer** (above). **The spiral is the ten single-character splash cards**, the design's call 8, not every file of `splash-art/normal`.
- **"Angela in the center" is Tetra** (design call 9).
- **Random order** of the fan pictures for every visit, as the owner said "pick random". The probe cannot fix it; the tests use seeds.
- **The artwork button stands under its title at the top**, not at the foot: the front of the spiral comes down to the foot of the screen and would cover it. The fan button stands at the foot, where the wall is clear.
- **The cursor label is `CREDIT`**, not `VIEW`: pressing a poster pins its credit, it opens no viewer.
- **The ring and the spiral's depth is scaled by `900 / h`** (never above 1): the stage's camera is a pixel camera with a fixed 1000 px lens, so the same depth would magnify a 1080 or 1440 high screen more. The near cards are now at the same fraction of the screen on 720, 900, 1080 and 1440 high screens (checked by a numeric sweep: bottoms at 0.93 to 0.97 of the height).
- **The compact screen** (a phone) gets a smaller rest for Tetra and the drone (`REST_COMPACT`, `REST_DRONE_COMPACT`): the wide one would put her half off the screen and the drone on her.
- **A fan picture's plate is real DOM** (for the keyboard, a screen reader and the takedown link), drawn only beside the poster under the pointer or the focused one.
