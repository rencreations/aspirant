## Decisions I took (nobody was awake to ask)

1. **The doll's colours are the picture the FBX carries itself**, not the separate base PNG. The PNG is a plain albedo (skin 196, 187, 186: grey) and comes out washed in any light that does not burn the hair; the FBX's own picture is warmer and brighter (skin 253, 227, 224) and is what the artist's viewer showed. The face is repainted with its skin. If the owner wants the PNG, `bake.py` takes the colour file as its fourth argument and `build.cjs` passes it: remove that argument.
2. **Bone names stay Meshy's** (`Spine02`, `Spine01`, `Spine`, `neck`), with the Mixamo map in `doll-rig.json`: the recon digest, its pose tables and the pose formula use them, and renaming would break every reader of the digest. 22 bones (two leaves folded into `Head`).
3. **The face is gated by a vertex attribute (`_FACE`), not by a UV mask.** On a chart soup atlas any dilation of a UV mask spreads across the gutters onto the hair, and the bangs share their position with the face behind them, so a feathered mask draws ghost eyes on the hair. The attribute is constant per triangle (vertices are split on the border), so there is no interpolation across the edge. The UV mask exists only inside the build, to repaint the maps, grown into free texels only.
4. **The face is drawn over the base, not instead of it**, so the edge needs no feather. Cheeks, blush and the angry red are in the shader.
5. **The face shell is smoothed and the lash pieces pressed flat** (see the doll README). The painted eyes had sculpted sockets and rims; with the paint gone they would be dents and fins that the drawn eyes do not follow. A 1.2 mm check against the source proves nothing else moved.
6. **No Meshopt, no Draco, no quantisation extension.** Plain GLTFLoader reads everything. Joints are bytes, weights normalised bytes, uvs normalised 16 bit; positions and normals stay float (a normalised byte vector of three is not 4 byte aligned, so it would not be valid glTF). The doll is 1.1 MB rather than the digest's 0.6: the vertices are 0.67 MB of it.
7. **The drone gets two more atlases, not a shader change.** Two 3 by 3 pages, 18 faces, the same layout as the shipped atlas, swapped at run time. No change to `drone-scene.ts`, `drone.glb` or the cache name.
8. **Kite aspect is the padded picture's** (0.7334), not the figure's (0.727).
9. **The thanks icon is a heart held up by two cupped hands** (a pair of praying hands read as a pen nib and a finger at this size, tried twice).

## What an actor needs (the contract)

**Doll** (`doll-rig.json` has every number). Load with a plain `GLTFLoader`; the one `SkinnedMesh` is `doll_body`, the material `doll` (MeshStandardMaterial: map, normalMap, and one picture for metalnessMap and roughnessMap). The file is 1.7 m tall, feet on y 0, front +Z, `Hips` is the root bone with no node above it that turns: scale the group by `h / 1.7`. Sockets `socket_hand_L`, `socket_hand_R` (the mitten's middle) and `socket_head_top`. The clip `walk` is in place: play it at rate r and move her at `0.5418 * r` m/s (in the file's units, scale with her); the hips are already lifted so a foot touches y 0. Poses are turns about the model's axes in the parent's bind frame (`poses.json`: stand, ride, scream, fall, sit, reach, run, held); arm stretch is `LeftArm.scale.y = 1.6` with `LeftHand.scale.y = 0.625`, never `ForeArm`. Light her with the recipe in `doll-rig.json` (a PMREM of RoomEnvironment 0.45, key 1.0, fill 0.35, rim 0.5, hemisphere 0.25, no tone mapping: the sum is about 1).

**The doll's face.** Wire the four chunks of `face/face.glsl` into `doll_body.material.onBeforeCompile` (`face/preview/main.js` is the reference, 15 lines): the vertex attribute is `_face`, the uniforms are `uFaceF` (a `Float32Array(56)`, the order of `presets.json` `names`), `uFaceFrame` (`[0.01025, 0.898, 0.1, 0.07307]`), `uIrisL` (`0xd9262f`), `uIrisR` (`0x5cbcf2`), `uInk` (`0x1d0f1d`); set `customProgramCacheKey`. An expression is a full set of the 55 floats (24 in `presets.json`); animate by lerping any two. Swap a glyph eye behind a blink. A blink is `blinkL`, `blinkR` from 0 to 1 on top of `open`. Comic cues (anger vein, hearts over the head, stars, the grawlix) are not in the shader: use the page's marks. The doll at 120 px tall and at 340 px reads (`doll-faces-small-120px.jpg`, `doll-faces-yaw0.jpg`).

**Gramophone** (`gramophone-rig.json`). Nodes `gramophone`, `cabinet`, `horn`, `crank` (rotation.z), `platter` (rotation.y, put the record on `anchor_record_seat` as its child), `soundbox` (quaternion about (0.38, 0, 0.93): 0 down, +50 degrees up, the needle rises 17 mm and moves 3 cm toward the middle), anchors `anchor_needle_tip` (child of `soundbox`), `anchor_record_seat` (child of `platter`), `anchor_crank_handle` (child of `crank`: a glove pinned to it moves on a circle of 0.060 about the pivot), `anchor_bell_mouth`, `anchor_elbow` (static). The default record is not in the file. Present it at a root yaw of 220 degrees (3.84 rad). Light it with a PMREM of RoomEnvironment plus a warm key and a cool rim; with no tone mapping, environment 0.8 and key 2.0. **Doll and gramophone want different amounts of environment light** (0.45 and 0.8): share one PMREM texture and give each material that texture as its own `envMap` with its own `envMapIntensity` (0.45 on the doll, 0.8 on the gramophone). In the three of this repository a material with `envMap === null` takes `scene.environmentIntensity` and ignores `envMapIntensity` (`WebGLRenderer.js`, around line 2735), so the scene-wide value cannot differ per material.

**Drone faces** (`drone-faces.json`). `material.map = atlas`, `uCell = face.uCell`; page A and page B are two textures. Respect `blinks: false`. **Marks** (`marks.json`): icons on a clear ground for the bubble. **Kite** (`kite.json`): the sail's aspect and the string's anchors in uv.

**Loading list (for K and the section owners).** All of these are `deferred` except what the first sections need: `doll.glb` (1.1 MB, first at the section where she appears, bar item with `weight` by bytes), `gramophone.glb` (0.79 MB, the music section, deferred), `drone-faces.webp` and `drone-faces-2.webp` (35 KB together; the loader and the first scenes use shocked and thanks, so wait for page A with the duo), `marks.webp` (25 KB, with the first bubble), `kite.webp` and `kite-bows.webp` (75 KB, the funny section). The files are new names: no cache-name bump. If one is rebuilt under the same name after it has shipped, bump `CACHE` in `lib/media-store.ts`.

## Numbers to tune

- Doll: `CREASE_DEGREES` 60 (vertices and shading), `RELIEF_OFFSET_CM`, the smoothing rounds (`smoothIterations` 8) and its factors (0.55, -0.58) in `page/convert.js`; `DEPTH_TOLERANCE` 1.6 and the hair-colour rule in `face-select.py`; `GROW` 6, `SKIN_BLUR` 70, `SKIN_RANGE` 9 in `bake.py`; the quality (0.85) in `convert()`.
- Face: every float in `params.json` and every preset in `presets.src.cjs`; the eye shape numbers in `gEye` (the sclera's 1.16 by 0.9, the iris radius 0.83, the lash 0.15), the mouth in `gMouth`. Run `build-face.cjs` then `preview.cjs` and look.
- Gramophone: `PIVOTS` and `ANCHORS` in `page/convert.js`, the lift axis, `FLIP_NORMAL_G`, the quality.

## Checks that run in the builds

Doll: the file's own checks (extensions, one skin, 22 joints, the clip, the attribute types), the loaded copy's (bones, nodes, no `Armature`, lit material, one packed picture, `_face` survives the loader, height 1.7 m, feet on y 0), and **the rebuilt doll against the source rig on four walk frames: at most 12 mm allowed, 1.2 mm found**. Face: `build-face.cjs` fails on a preset naming an unknown float, a value outside its range, or a shader macro that does not exist. Gramophone: the horn is +X, the crank on -Z, each pivot inside 4 mm of its part's box, the crank's box turns on a circle about its pivot, the platter does not wander, the file is under 1.3 MB. Drone faces: every face on the real drone at rest, at blink 0.6 and with the look slid.

## Failures and gaps (said plainly)

- **"Hang by one hand" is not achievable** with this doll (the brief lists it). The arm straight up (Z +100 to +150 degrees, stretched 1.6, hips rolled 28 degrees either way) is inside the hair mass from the front, the three-quarter and the side. The pose that is in `poses.json` is `reach` (forward and up, stretched, the hand about at the top of the head). Hold her by the thing she holds: put the edge at `socket_hand_L` and let the legs dangle (`held`), or shoot from below. Details in `poses.json` (`notAchievable`).
- **No Blender pass.** The brief asked for a Blender pass to pick the face polygons. I did it with a script (`face-select.py`: depth layer, colour, neighbour vote, relief by dark paint) and checked it by rendering the selection in magenta over the base picture at 0, 30 and 60 degrees, then proved it by baking and looking at the empty face. No triangle was picked by hand; `curation/face-edits.json` is the place if the owner wants one added or removed.
- **The face shows its low polygon count at a very close view** (a 1440 by 900 close-up with the face at 60 percent of the height): faint facets on the forehead and cheeks, softened by smoothing the normals of the face's inner vertices, and a ridge line where the face region meets the bangs above the eyes (`doll-face-large-*.jpg`). Lashes drawn by the shader are even; the repainted skin is not soft or blurred.
- **The outputs of the builds are not Prettier-formatted** (they are written with `JSON.stringify`): `pnpm check` fails on them after a rebuild until the commit hook or `npx prettier --write` formats them. I formatted the ones in the repository now.
- **I ran `prettier --write scripts/home` once by mistake**, which includes the folders of M1 and D (`scripts/home/models/*.mjs`, `lib/`, `verify/`, `media/`) while they were working. It is formatting only (Prettier 3 rewrites only files that differ). If a tool of theirs complained of a changed file, that is why.

- **No vitest.** The vitest config of the repository includes only `apps/web/src/**/*.test.ts`, and this wave allowed no TypeScript in `apps/web/src`. The pure functions I wrote (the weld, the crease normals, the Taubin smoothing, the clip fit, the SDF shapes) are browser and shader code in `scripts/`; they are proven by the checks above, not by unit tests. When the actors move the face's float packing and the pose formula into `features/home/doll/`, those two want unit tests.
- **knip fails** on the page folders: `three` is "unlisted" in `scripts/home/drone/page/main.js`, `scripts/home/models/doll/page/*.js`, `scripts/home/models/doll/face/preview/main.js` and `scripts/home/models/gramophone/page/*.js` (and in M1's `scripts/home/models/verify/page/stage.js`): these run in a browser with an import map. The root `knip.json` (not mine) has the three about/ page folders in its `ignore` list: add `"scripts/home/**/page/**"` and `"scripts/home/models/doll/face/preview/**"`.
- **A faint ridge line across the forehead** (where the face region meets the bangs) shows at 30 to 70 degrees; the drawn brows cover most of it. The face shell's border is not smoothed on purpose.
- **Open-eye to glyph-eye blends ghost** (both are drawn half transparent). Hide the swap behind a blink.
- **The doll's size** is 1.1 MB, above the digest's estimate; 0.67 MB of it is geometry (14.5k vertices of 40 bytes). Raising `CREASE_DEGREES` to 80 saves about 80 KB.
- **Not looked at:** Firefox and Safari, a real weak machine, the model under the home's shared renderer and post passes, the walk in motion on a floor (the speed is measured from the planted foot; the feet were checked on frames, not in a loop), the doll's face under a light rig other than the recipe.
