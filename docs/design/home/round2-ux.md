## 3. Defects found and fixed

1. **The Selena sky stayed on over the whole top of the page after a scroll up.** Seen as a dark navy ground under dark words on the aspirants and rows (unreadable) after walking up from anywhere below the list. Cause: `put(el, "visibility", "visible")` on a layer in a `slotBelow`, which a hidden parent cannot hide (an explicit `visible` wins over an inherited `hidden`). The section's own last update on the way up leaves the sky "visible". Fixed with `inherit` for the layers of the Selena (drawer, sky, white) and the list (window, items, panel): `sections/selena/runtime.ts`, `sections/list/runtime.ts`. Looked at: after `seek(20)` and a wheel up to 0.8 the ground is light again (`c5-a.png`). **Rule for the next person: never write `visibility: visible` on a layer in `below`; write `inherit`.**
2. **The aspirants' words (including the "About Aspirant" link) were not in the page's tree or the tab order until the hero's scene had played** (`visibility: hidden` in `sections/aspirants/runtime.ts`). A reader or a keyboard visitor could not reach them at the top. Now they take `pointer-events: none` while out and stay in the tree. The four lines carry `data-home-wait`, and a focus on one of them that has not come in yet (opacity under 5 percent along its parents) moves the page to where it shows (`home-stage.tsx`, `shownNow`), as a focus into a section that is off already did. A first, wider version of this rule (any invisible control of a section that is on) was dropped: it also fires for controls that are hidden by design in a section that is on (the list's names, the strip's buttons, the bend's plates), which have their own rules. Looked at with `focusjump.cjs`.
3. `sections/rows/preload.test.ts` still asked for the old `strip-1280.mp4`; the trailer is the stream item `stream:home-trailer` now.
4. `e2e/home.spec.ts` was written for round one: the total length (55 to 70, now 25 to 40), the one canvas (now two: stage and backdrop, both `aria-hidden`), the strip's "See All" link (now `/collection/video/films/`). Updated. Three new specs: a seek puts every scene at rest; the thresholds and joints are in order inside the page; a scene fires at its threshold, holds the scroll (the page moves under 0.05 screen while it runs, the wheel does nothing), lets go, and plays back going up.
5. **knip**: eight unused exports and one type, from the wall, rows and strip work: `GRID_SIZE`, `sliceAt`, `coverPlan`, `ASPIRANTS_SCREENS`, `ASPIRANTS_CLOSE_AT`, `FILM_END_U`, `BOOST_GAIN` lost their `export`; `rowsScale` and `RowsScale` (dead since the rows and the wall became one plane) were deleted from `sections/strip/film-state.ts`.
6. `scripts/qa/probes/home.cjs` waits for plays and the snap before it goes on (steps of at most 800 px: bigger is a flick the kernel limits); `home-s1.cjs` seeks to the wall rest at 1.0 (it was 4.6, the round one wall); `home-plays.cjs` is new.

## 4. UX audit

- **Visibility, all sections**: grepped every write of `visibility` in the home. Every remaining `"visible"` is on a node of the text layer (aspirants, archive, selena's front, music's CSS, `below: null` for the music) or on the backdrop's own canvas. None is in a `below` layer any more.
- **Hover, press, focus**: the e2e specs for names, tab order, focus marks, "a control names the place where it shows", "moves the page there" and "first and last control shows" pass on desktop, tablet and phone (the last two failed before fix 2 and the e2e update).
- **Keyboard**: Space walks the whole page (section 1). A focus from the top into a control of a section that is not on, or is on but has not shown it yet, moves the page to the place where it shows, and no scene fires on the way (`requestSnap`).
- **Back and Forward**: Back from `/about` opens the home at rest with no second opening (e2e); a reload part way down opens at the top (e2e). Not tried: a Forward after Back.
- **Backdrop canvas**: no WebGL, a lost context or a context gone for good is a no-op that keeps the plain grounds (`fx/backdrop/renderer.ts`, `onGone`). The streamed trailer restarts a stalled or dropped download from where it stood (R-A4 measured it). Both read in the code, not exercised again by me.
- **Loading states of deferred things**: the strip's window, the music and the voice lines were not exercised on a throttled link by me.
- **Contrast**: read in the sheets, nothing unreadable after fix 1.
- **Touch targets**: the phone sheets show the list names, the controls of the strip and the buttons at a normal size; not measured one by one.
- **Scroll cue**: a hairline in the lower right appears after 1.8 s at a rest (`rest-cue.ts`). It has no words. See "left".
