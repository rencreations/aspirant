# Home sections: voices and music (workstream S5)

Two sections that share one pair of actors, Guinevere's two drones (the duo). `sections/voices/` (2.4 screens, lead 0.5, tail 0.5) is the character chooser, the curated voice lines and the splash art, with the pair dancing on a line. `sections/music/` (2.8 screens, lead 0.5, tail 0.5) is the pair taking the scene apart and bringing a gramophone and a crate of records, a locked screen, a record picked, the song, comic notes out of the horn. Request: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-home-page-request.md` ("Voicelines" and "Music"). Actors: `actor-duo.md`, `actor-gramophone.md` (gramophone and records), `fx-marks.md` (notes, hearts). Data: `media.md` (`public/data/home/voices.json`).

No section length changed. Both grounds are daybreak, nav theme daybreak.

## 3. The lock (nobody is trapped)

- The hold is taken once per visit at the lock point (a scroll target crossing `local` 0.22 going down, a step of less than 0.25), and only when the gramophone, the crate and the covers have arrived (`propsReady`). With them late the scroll is never held: a visitor would wait on a line of text. The scene then comes in by the scroll and stands when the files arrive. Measured with the gramophone held back 30 s: the page scrolled on freely.
- Skipped when `sessionStorage["aspirants:intro"]` is `off` (tests, probes), as every hold of the site.
- The ways out, tested with a real wheel and with real touch events: a fresh wheel or touch gesture after the scene stands (or after a pick); a scroll key (Space, PageDown, ArrowDown, End; Space on a focused button presses it and does not count); "Continue"; the song heard for 6 s; the kernel's own limit of 30 s. `intent.ts` is the gate. A gesture is a run of events with no gap over 450 ms (a finger going down always begins one). A gesture that began before the scene stood, or within `GRACE_MS` 1400 ms after it, never counts: a trackpad goes on sending events for a second after the fingers lift, and a visitor who keeps swiping through the arrival (each touch is a new gesture) must see the finished scene before a swipe lets them go. A fresh one counts at 100 px of wheel or 36 px of touch. A steady wheel through the five seconds of the arrival does not release it (measured: the scroll stays at 36.73 for 5.6 s of steady wheel). The runtime's listeners are added before the hold's own, so the first event of the new gesture goes through as the hold is let go.
- The song stops, the disc goes home and the hold is let go as soon as the exit begins (`exit > 0`) and in `leave`, `dispose` and a jump past. A jump into the section starts nothing: a sound needs a press.
- **A finger that goes down on a sleeve picks it only if it lifts as a tap** (`isTap`: within 12 px and 600 ms; a cancel never counts). The pointer hub reports a press at pointer-down, and the hold stops the browser from taking a drag for a scroll, so without this a swipe that begins on the crate picked a record (seen in the browser with a real touch drag). A mouse picks at the press. A tap picks on the lift, inside a real gesture.

## 4. Sound

- Every voice line and every song starts inside a real press (a click, a tap, a key, or the hidden keys). With the site's sound off the press asks first (`withSound`: "Turn sound on", "Keep it off", "Do not ask again"); the site's sound button mutes everything through the engine.
- A sleeve press is found by the pointer hub in the frame loop, not inside the event, so the section unlocks the audio on any `pointerdown` while the scene is on.
- Voices: `playVoice(libUrl(key), { gainDb: g })`. `playVoice` stops the line that plays and announces "stopped" at once, before it announces the new one, so a row is marked after the call has begun (found in the browser: switching lines mid-play left the new row idle and the pair still).
- Music: `audio.playMusic(track.audio, { loop: false, volume: 0.7, fadeMs: 1200 })`, `audio.stopMusic(600)`. The track's `ended` event moves the scheduler to `ended` (needle up, crank stops, the disc waits).

## 9. How to look

`node scripts/qa/probes/home-s5.cjs <outFolder>` (`BASE=http://localhost:3100 SIZES=1440x900,390x844`). It checks and prints ok or FAIL: a line plays and its row says so, the pair dances, switching lines, a second press stops, tabs and arrow keys, the Arabic line, the three joints, the props arrive, a sleeve is found with the pointer (a tap on a phone), the song plays on the platter, "Another record", the crank key, leaving mid-song, a jump back in, the lock with a real wheel (held, steady wheel does not carry through, Continue shows, a fresh gesture lets go) and the lock with the gramophone held back. To test the real hold from a page opened with the intro off, set `sessionStorage["aspirants:intro"]` to `on` after the page has opened (the section reads it every frame).

## 10. Calls made without the owner

1. The page is held when the scroll target crosses `local` 0.22, not on an exact screen, and only once the props are there (see 3).
2. "Continue" is a text button that appears while the page is held and scrolls on past the scene (to `local` 1.02). A fresh scroll gesture also lets go: the owner asked for a lock, and a lock that cannot be left is a trap.
3. Seven sleeves, because `music.json` has seven tracks with a cover. The crate takes up to twelve.
   3b. The narrow layout of the voices section is the CSS breakpoint's own, 900 px inclusive (`NARROW_MAX` in `choreo.ts`, a test holds 900 narrow and 901 wide). A narrow screen that is also under 780 px tall gets tighter rows so the eighth row clears the button.
4. The song starts when the needle is down and the platter is up to speed, 5 to 6 seconds after the press (earlier it waited for the drone to be home, 2 seconds more).
5. One status line, three words in turn: "Winding up the gramophone" (props coming), "Pick a record", then "Picked <title>" and "Now playing <title>". The functional words are in `MUSIC_UI` and `VOICES_UI` (names of controls, not the owner's copy).
6. The hearts between the pair are the section's own (a timer of 1.15 s while a line plays), not a cue of the duo. The duo's `dance` has the fist bump, the turns and the spins.
7. The two titles of the joint do not overlap: the music title fades in from `local` 0, the voices ones are gone at its 1.
8. The art frame is a rounded 16 by 9 stage at the right with the picture cropped by a hand chosen focus point (`ART_POSITION`); the lines' column is half the width at most. On a phone the art is a band under the chooser.
9. The preview of other languages is one line each, as asked. The Japanese line also shows its English translation under it (the data has it); the others do not.
10. When a song ends by itself the needle goes up, the crank stops and the disc stays on the platter. The sleeves stay quiet (they can be pressed only while the crate waits) and "Another record" is the way out: it puts the disc back and opens the crate. A pick made while a record is out is queued behind it, and starts from the frame loop once the crate is free (checked: lift, return, toCrate, fetch, carry, place, needle, back, playing).
11. The `Insight` badge in the screenshots is the Next.js development overlay, not the page.

## 11. Not done, not verified

- Firefox, Safari, a real phone (touch was tested with Chrome's touch emulation: a tap, and a synthesized swipe for the lock), a weak machine, the production build (all numbers are the dev server's). No sound was listened to: playback was seen through the engine's state (the audio element plays, time advances, pauses after the fade), not heard.
- The pair is drawn under the lit cast (the gramophone and the crate), so the right drone is half hidden behind the gramophone's base when it cranks and the disc leaves the glove at a distance in the carry. That is the duo's pass order (20, under the cast's 30), an actor limit; the crank glove is pinned to the handle.
- The first frame of the voices section reads grey in a seek sheet: the ground cross fades over 0.5 screens centred on the joint with Selena's section. White at J9 is the Selena section's job (her stub was still a stub when this was written).
- The probe was run against the dev server while other helpers edited it: three runs were broken by a neighbour's half written file (not mine) and repeated.
- If the kernel's 30 s safety net lets go of the hold, the section's own `release` stays set, so `data-held` (and the "Continue" button) keep showing on a page that already scrolls freely. Continue still works. Cosmetic, left.
- "Continue" moves the page to `local` 1.02 in about 0.4 s (the kernel's `scroll.to` has no duration), so the exit plays as a blur. It is a skip button.
- The records actor's hit shape is thin at the top of a sleeve: at 1706 x 950 the top 30 px of the sleeves did not answer to the pointer (hover and press) while the body did. That is the actor's, not changed here.
- The keyboard order was walked with Tab, the arrow keys and Enter (the chooser, the play buttons, the hidden record keys, "Turn the crank", "See all music"); a screen reader was not used. Axe (wcag2a, 2aa, 21aa) found 0 violations in the voices text, the music text and the music text with a key focused, at 1440 x 900.
- Resize in the middle of a scene was not tested: resizing changes the page's screen unit, so the scroll position lands in another section, which is the kernel's matter. The layout of both sections is a pure function of the viewport each frame, and the art frame has a ResizeObserver. Sizes looked at: 1440 x 900, 1706 x 950, 900 x 700, 768 x 1024 (voices only: a neighbour's build error covered the music frame) and 390 x 844.
- A tap on a touch page that lands 16 ms after a mouse move missed the sleeve once (the same tap after a pause, or with no mouse, picks). A person never does that, so it was left; the probe pauses as a person would.
- No quality or device gating anywhere. Measured on the dev server (Chrome, real GPU, 1440 x 900): `update` costs 0.05 ms in voices while a line plays and the pair dances, 0.10 ms in music with the song playing, and the page runs at 60 fps with all of it on screen.
