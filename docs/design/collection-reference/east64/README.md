# East64 home page: the reference frames

The owner asked (11 Nov 2026) for Splash art (`/collection/arts`) to copy the home page of <https://www.east64films.com/>. These frames were recorded from that page in real Chrome on 11 Nov 2026 at 1440x900, 1920x1080, 1024x768, 820x1180, 2560x1440 and a 390x844 touch phone (2x). They are for looking at. Nothing of the reference's code, art, fonts or wording is used: the site rebuilds the structure and the motion with its own pictures, type and words. The pictures in the frames are the reference's own and are kept here small, as study notes only.

How it works, with the measured numbers and a build recipe: [`docs/design/arts-reference.md`](../../arts-reference.md). Time stamps on the sheets are in milliseconds from the start of the action, taken from the browser's screencast. The recording browser ran faster than 60 Hz, so the step looks quicker here than on a normal screen: section 2.3 of that file has the 60 Hz timings.
